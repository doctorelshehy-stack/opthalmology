Lagophthalmos

The lids do
not close

Incomplete closure of the palpebral fissure on voluntary or involuntary closure. Bell's phenomenon protects the eye during sleep.

Definition

Lagophthalmos means incomplete closure of the palpebral fissure on closure (voluntary or involuntary) of the eyelids.

Bell's phenomenon

A protective mechanism in which rolling up of the eye during sleep diminishes the effect of incomplete closure of the palpebral fissure.

Degrees

  • Mild: the patient can close the lids voluntarily by forcible squeezing of the lids, but the lids remain open during sleep.
  • Severe: the palpebral fissure does not close even with forcible squeezing of the lids.

The exposed interpalpebral zone puts the cornea at risk of exposure keratopathy.
