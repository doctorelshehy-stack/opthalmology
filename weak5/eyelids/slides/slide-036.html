Ectropion

The margin
rolls outward

Rolling out (outward rotation) of the lid margin. Degrees are defined by how much of the lid and conjunctiva is exposed.

Degrees

  1. Mild: the lacrimal punctum is visible without pulling the lid.
  2. Moderate: the tarsal conjunctiva is exposed.
  3. Severe: a completely everted lid with exposure of the conjunctival fornix.

Etiological types

  • 1 · Congenital.
  • 2 · Acquired:
  • a · Cicatricial.
  • b · Senile (involutional).
  • c · Mechanical.
  • d · Paralytic.

Outward rotation exposes the tarsal conjunctiva and displaces the punctum.
