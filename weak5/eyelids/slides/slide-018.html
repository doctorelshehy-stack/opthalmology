Blepharitis · Complications Eyelids · Ophthalmology

Complications

Conjunctiva, cornea,
lacrimal and eyelid

Chronic marginal inflammation damages the ocular surface and the lid architecture — producing trichiasis, madarosis, ptylosis and recurrent styes.

Conjunctiva

Chronic conjunctivitis.

Cornea

  • Marginal corneal ulcers.
  • Superficial punctate keratitis.

Lacrimal

Punctal occlusion.

Eyelid

  1. Trichiasis — scarring at the lash roots (healed ulcer).
  2. Madarosis — destruction of hair follicles.
  3. Ptylosis — destruction of the sharp posterior border, causing epiphora and initiating the vicious circle of epiphora → eczema → ectropion.
  4. Multiple styes.
