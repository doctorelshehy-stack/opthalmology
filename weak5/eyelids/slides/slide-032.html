Management · trichiasis

Pure trichiasis
is surgical

Pure trichiasis without entropion is treated surgically. Trichiasis with entropion is treated by correcting the entropion.

Pure trichiasis — without entropion

Treated surgically:

  • Removal of the lash-bearing area: indicated for partial pure trichiasis.
  • Direct internal removal of the lash roots through an eyelid crease incision.

Trichiasis with entropion

Treatment of the entropion — see below.

The presence of entropion changes the operation: the lid margin itself must be repositioned, not simply the lashes removed.
