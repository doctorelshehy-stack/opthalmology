Hordeolum internum

The inner stye

Acute suppurative inflammation of the meibomian gland. It may be primary or secondary to infection of a chalazion.

Definition and types

  • Acute, suppurative inflammation of the meibomian gland.
  • May be primary, or secondary due to infection of a chalazion (acute chalazion).

Etiology

Causative organisms and predisposing factors: as in blepharitis.

Clinical picture

  • The same as in stye, but pain is more severe — the meibomian gland is larger and embedded in tarsus, making pus under tension.
  • The swelling is away from the lid margin and not related to the eyelashes.
  • A yellow spot appears on the conjunctival side.
Treatment: like that for stye, but evacuation may be needed through a vertical or cruciate incision on the conjunctival side.
