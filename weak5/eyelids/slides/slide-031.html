Management · rubbing lashes

Temporary epilation,
permanent destruction

Epilation is temporary: stronger lashes regrow within 2–3 weeks. Permanent options destroy the involved lashes.

Temporary measure · epilation

It is always followed by regrowth of stronger lashes within 2–3 weeks.

Permanent measures

Destruction of the involved lashes by either of the following:

  1. Electrolysis.
  2. Argon laser ablation.
  3. Diathermy and cryoapplication — these may result in disfigurement and depigmentation of the lid margin.
Permanent destruction is reserved for recurrent or troublesome lashes; the trade-off is the risk of lid-margin scarring and depigmentation.
