Ectropion · senile and mechanical

Gravity, laxity
and weight

Senile ectropion is the most common type, affecting the lower lid with redundant skin. Mechanical ectropion is caused by increased lid weight.

Senile (involutional) ectropion

Most common type.

  • Usually affects the lower lid due to gravity with redundant skin.
  • Pathogenesis: same as involutional entropion; however, the lower eyelid is well supported by the globe.

Lines of treatment

  • Mild: cautery puncture (conjunctival side) to form a scar that everts the lid margin inwards.
  • Moderate: Snellen's sutures.
  • Severe: Dimmer's modification of Kuhnt operation.

Mechanical ectropion

Due to an increase in the weight of the lower eyelid from a large tumour or multiple chalazia.

Treatment: of the cause.

Removing the weight restores lid position; the lid itself may not need a tightening procedure.
