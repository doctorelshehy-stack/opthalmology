Ectropion · cicatricial

Scar in the
anterior lamella

Cicatricial ectropion is caused by scarring of the skin. Small scars are managed with V-Y; large scars require Z-plasty or grafting.

Causes · scar in the skin

  • Extensive heat or chemical burns of the face.
  • Skin disease: eczema, malignant tumours, scleroderma.
  • Postoperative scars or lacerated wounds of the eyelid.

Lines of treatment

Protect the cornea from exposure until the deformity is corrected surgically by:

  • Small scar: V-Y operation.
  • Large scar: Z-plasty, or removal of the scar tissue followed by eyelid grafting.
The priority is corneal protection while awaiting definitive scar release and resurfacing.
