Complications

A cycle of
surface damage

Chronic rubbing damages the conjunctiva and cornea, producing recurrent ulceration, opacification and vascularisation.

Conjunctival

  • Chronic conjunctivitis.
  • Conjunctival ulceration.
  • Epithelial plaque.

Corneal

  • Recurrent ulceration, which may be followed by:
  • Opacification.
  • Superficial vascularisation.
  • Epithelial plaque.
The complications are the result of continuous mechanical trauma; definitive treatment therefore requires removal or redirection of the offending lashes, not just lubrication.
