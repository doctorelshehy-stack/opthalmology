Definition

Blepharitis

Chronic inflammation of the eyelid margin. There are three main types: anterior, posterior and mixed.

1 · Anterior blepharitis

At the lash base

Inflammation affects the skin around the base of the eyelashes.

Usually subdivided into staphylococcal and seborrhoeic variants.

2 · Posterior blepharitis

Meibomian glands

Inflammation affects the meibomian glands, causing meibomian gland dysfunction.

3 · Mixed blepharitis

Both compartments

A combination of both anterior and posterior blepharitis.

The distinction matters because anterior disease targets the lash follicles and skin, while posterior disease targets the meibomian glands and the ocular surface lipid layer.
