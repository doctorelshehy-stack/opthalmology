Lashes · Rubbing versus trichiasis Eyelids · Ophthalmology

Definitions

Four lashes
or more

The distinction is numerical and clinical: four or fewer inward misdirected lashes are “rubbing lashes”; more than four directed posteriorly constitute trichiasis.

Rubbing lashes

4 or fewer

Refers to 4 or less inward misdirected lashes rubbing against the cornea or bulbar conjunctiva.

Trichiasis

More than 4

An acquired condition in which more than 4 lashes are directed posteriorly and rub against the cornea or bulbar conjunctiva.

Common ground: both produce corneal and conjunctival irritation, and both may coexist with entropion.
Key difference: trichiasis is acquired and implies a larger burden of misdirected lashes; rubbing lashes are a smaller, focal problem.
