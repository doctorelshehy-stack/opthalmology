Distichiasis

An extra row

A usually congenital abnormality in which an extra row of lashes arises from the lid margin behind the grey line, in the place of the meibomian gland orifices.

Definition

An abnormality, usually congenital, in which an extra row of lashes arises from the lid margin behind the grey line, in the place of the meibomian gland orifices.

Treatment

  • Asymptomatic cases: no treatment.
  • Complicated cases: destruction of ectopic lashes by:
  1. Direct removal.
  2. Cryotherapy on the anterior surface of the tarsus after splitting the anterior from the posterior lamellae at the grey line.

The extra row emerges behind the grey line, where meibomian orifices normally open.
