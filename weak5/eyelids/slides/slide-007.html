Eyelids · Layers Eyelids · Ophthalmology

Layers 1–4

From thin skin
to the muscle plane

The skin is thin and loosely adherent; the subcutaneous layer contains no fat; the muscle layer combines striated and non-striated muscles.

1 · The skin

Thin, elastic and loosely adherent to underlying tissues.

2 · Subcutaneous areolar tissue

Contains no fat.

3 · The muscle layer

  • Striated (voluntary) muscles: orbicularis oculi and levator palpebrae superioris.
  • Non-striated muscles: Müller's muscle in the upper eyelid and the lower eyelid retractors in the lower eyelid.

4 · Submuscular areolar tissue

  • Lies between the orbicularis muscle and the tarsal plate.
  • Contains the peripheral arterial arcade and the main sensory nerves to the eyelid.
  • Therefore local anaesthetic must be injected deep to the orbicularis.
