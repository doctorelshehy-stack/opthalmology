Ectropion · clinical picture

Epiphora, eczema
and a vicious circle

Punctal eversion causes epiphora; the resulting skin eczema scars and pulls the lid further out, worsening the epiphora.

Clinical picture

Depends on the severity and duration of ectropion.

  • Epiphora: due to eversion of the lacrimal punctum. This leads to an eczematous reaction of the skin of the lower lid, followed by scarring, more ectropion, then more epiphora — a vicious circle.
  • Chronic conjunctival hyperaemia, dryness and hypertrophy.
  • Lagophthalmos with exposure keratopathy in severe paralytic cases.

Congenital ectropion

Rare, dated since birth. Usually associated with other lid anomalies, as in blepharophimosis syndrome.

The self-reinforcing cycle of epiphora, skin eczema, scarring and further ectropion.
