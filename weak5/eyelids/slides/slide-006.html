Minute anatomy

Six layers,
outside inwards

The eyelid is classically described in six layers, from skin to palpebral conjunctiva. Each layer has a different surgical behaviour.

Skin
Subcutaneous areolar tissue
Muscle layer
Submuscular areolar tissue
Fibrous layer · tarsus + septum
Palpebral conjunctiva
  1. The skin.
  2. The subcutaneous areolar tissue.
  3. The muscle layer.
  4. The submuscular areolar tissue.
  5. The fibrous layer — tarsus and orbital septum.
  6. The conjunctiva — palpebral portion.
The layers are not just descriptive: the grey line, tarsus and orbital septum define the surgical planes of the lid.
