Margin · 1 of 2

Lacrimal and
ciliary parts

The free margin is 2–3 mm wide. The lacrimal papilla and punctum divide it into a medial lacrimal part and a lateral ciliary part.

General

The free margin of the lid. It is 2–3 mm in width.

1 · Lacrimal part — medial 1/6

  • From the punctum to the medial canthus.
  • Where there are no cilia and no tarsal glands.
  • Contains the lacrimal canaliculus.

2 · Ciliary part — lateral 5/6

From the punctum to the lateral canthus.

The punctum marks the boundary between the lacrimal and ciliary parts.
