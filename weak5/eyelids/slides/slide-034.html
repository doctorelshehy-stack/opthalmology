Entropion · types 2–3

Scarring versus
spasm

Cicatricial entropion follows cicatrizing lid-margin disease. Spastic entropion is a reflex in young patients with acute ocular inflammation or chronic corneal irritation.

2 · Cicatricial entropion

Causes are as for mal-directed lashes.

Treatment: mainly surgical.

May affect both lids, with scarring of the conjunctiva and deformity of the tarsus — a shortage of the posterior lid lamella.

3 · Spastic entropion

Pathogenesis: occurs in young age due to spasm of Riolan's muscle as a reflex (blepharospasm) of acute ocular inflammation or chronic corneal irritation.

More common in the lower lid — the tarsus is small and thin and rolls easily.

Lines of treatment of spastic entropion

  1. Removal or treatment of the predisposing cause.
  2. Temporary measures to correct the lid margin.
  • T-shaped adhesive plaster.
  • Botulinum toxin injection to orbicularis to weaken it (chemical denervation).
  • Everting sutures: 3–4 mattress sutures, starting at the lower fornix and tied on the skin below the eyelid margin.

Permanent measures

Only for chronic resistant cases.

Skin and muscle operation: excision of an elliptical area of skin with the underlying muscle of Riolan, close to the lid margin.
