Blepharitis · Clinical picture Eyelids · Ophthalmology

Symptoms and signs

Burning, crusts
and lashes

Symptoms are worse in the morning. Squamous disease shows greasy scales; ulcerative disease shows yellow crusts that leave bleeding ulcers when removed.

Symptoms

  • Burning sensation that is worse in the morning.
  • Itching.
  • Lacrimation.
  • Photophobia.
  • Frequent falling of hair.

Signs · squamous blepharitis

Greasy, greyish-white scales between the lashes.

Signs · ulcerative blepharitis

Lashes are glued together by yellow crusts. When the crusts are removed, they leave minute bleeding ulcers on the margin.
