Entropion

The margin
rolls inward

Rolling in of the lid margin with eyelashes rubbing against the eyeball. The four etiological types are congenital, cicatricial, spastic and senile.

Definition

Usually defined as rolling in of the lid margin with eyelashes rubbing against the eyeball.

Etiological types

  1. Congenital.
  2. Cicatricial.
  3. Spastic.
  4. Senile (involutional).
Clinical picture: as in acquired mal-directed lashes.

1 · Congenital entropion

Common in the lower eyelid. Causes: hypertrophy of the orbicularis; dehiscence or absence of the lower lid retractors.

Treatment: reattachment of lower lid retractors to the lower tarsal border.

Inward rotation brings the lashes against the cornea and bulbar conjunctiva.
