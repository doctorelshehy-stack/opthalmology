Assessment

Measure the MRD
and levator function

Margin-to-reflex distance grades the amount of ptosis; levator function determines the choice of operation.

1 · Determine the amount of ptosis

Margin-to-reflex distance (MRD): from the centre of the upper lid margin to the corneal reflex. More accurate than measuring the palpebral fissure width.

MildModerateSevere
Drooping 1–2 mmDrooping 3 mmDrooping > 4 mm

2 · Determine levator function power

Technique: correct the head position, ask the patient to look down and fix the eyebrow against the superior orbital margin with the thumb to prevent elevation by frontalis, then ask the patient to look up and measure the elevation of the lid margin (mm) with a ruler.

PoorFairGoodNormal
4 mm or less5–11 mm11–14 mm15 mm or more
The two measurements answer different questions: MRD quantifies the ptosis; levator function determines whether the levator can be strengthened or must be bypassed.
