Layer 5

Tarsus, meibomian
glands and septum

The fibrous layer consists of the tarsal plates, the meibomian glands embedded within them, and the orbital septum.

1 · Tarsal plates

  • Dense fibrous tissue forming the skeleton of the lids.
  • Upper tarsus height 8–12 mm; lower about 4–5 mm.
  • Laterally and medially, they fuse with the medial and lateral palpebral ligaments (canthal tendons).

2 · Meibomian (tarsal) glands

  • Elongated sebaceous glands embedded within the tarsal plate.
  • 30 in the upper lid and 25 in the lower lid.
  • Orifices open on the lid margin behind the grey line.
  • Form an oily film over the moistened cornea to retard evaporation of tears and prevent overflow over the skin.

3 · Orbital septum

  • Separates the eyelids from the orbit.
  • Arises from the periosteum of the orbital rim and attaches to the levator aponeurosis in the upper lid and the lower tarsal border in the lower lid.
  • Acts as a diaphragm keeping fat within the orbit.
