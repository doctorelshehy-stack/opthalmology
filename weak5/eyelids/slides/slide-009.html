Conjunctiva and orbicularis

The inner lining
and the closing muscle

The palpebral conjunctiva is firmly adherent to tarsus. The orbicularis has orbital, palpebral and lacrimal parts with distinct functions.

6 · Palpebral conjunctiva

  • Thin and vascular; firmly adherent to the tarsus.
  • 2 mm above the lid margin it shows a horizontal groove — the sulcus subtarsalis.

Orbicularis oculi

  • Orbital part: responsible for tight closure of the eyelids.
  • Palpebral part: responsible for gentle eyelid closure; subdivided into preseptal (overlying orbital septum) and pretarsal (overlying tarsus).
  • Muscle of Riolan (pars ciliaris): spreads along the lid margin and keeps the eyelid in apposition with the globe.
  • Lacrimal part — Horner's muscle (pars lacrimalis): responsible for the lacrimal pump.
The orbicularis oculi is supplied by the facial nerve (7th nerve). Paralysis leads to lagophthalmos, epiphora and paralytic ectropion.
