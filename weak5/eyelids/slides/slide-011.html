Non-striated muscle

Müller's muscle
and lower retractors

Müller's muscle sustains upper lid elevation and is sympathetically innervated. The lower lid retractors give the lower lid vertical stability.

1 · Müller's muscle

  • Action: sustains elevation of the upper eyelid.
  • Course: descends behind the levator aponeurosis.
  • Origin: posterior aspect of the levator muscle.
  • Insertion: superior tarsal border.
Innervated by autonomic sympathetic nerves; paralysis leads to Horner's syndrome.

2 · Retractors of the lower lid

In down-gaze, the inferior rectus retracts the lower lid through the lower lid retractors, which are attached to the inferior border of the tarsus of the lower lid, giving the lid its vertical stability.
