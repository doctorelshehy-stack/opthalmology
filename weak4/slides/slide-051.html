Hordeolum externum

The stye

Acute suppurative inflammation of the Zeiss gland. Staphylococcus aureus is the causative organism; predisposing factors are as in blepharitis.

Etiology

  • Predisposing factors: as in blepharitis.
  • Causative agent: Staphylococcus aureus.

Clinical picture

  • Symptoms: painful red swelling at the eyelid margin.
  • Signs: red tender swelling at the root of a lash; a yellow point forms and may become an abscess.

Treatment

  1. Treatment of the predisposing factors.
  2. Warm, moist compresses and topical antibiotics (drops and ointment).
  3. Systemic antibiotics in severe cases complicated by cellulitis.
  4. If pointing: drain pus by epilation of the related eyelash with a horizontal incision.
Do not press to evacuate a stye. The eyelid is a dangerous area; pressure may spread infection and cause orbital cellulitis or cavernous sinus thrombosis.
