Papilledema · Definition Week 4 · Ophthalmology

Definition

Papilledema

Passive, non-inflammatory oedema of the optic disc resulting from increased intracranial pressure.

Etiology

  1. Benign intracranial hypertension (BIH) — previously pseudo-tumour cerebri. More common in females in the second or third decade of life.
  2. Intracranial tumours.
  3. Other intracranial causes — e.g. cavernous sinus thrombosis, subarachnoid haemorrhage.

Key distinction

Papilledema is passive and non-inflammatory. It is a sign of raised intracranial pressure, not a primary inflammation of the disc.

BIH is more common in females in the second or third decade of life.
