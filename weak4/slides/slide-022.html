Retina · Retinal vascular disorders

Retinopathy of toxemia of pregnancy

This occurs late in pregnancy after 20th week. The blood pressure is markedly elevated.

Symptoms
  • No symptoms in mild cases.
  • Metamorphopsia and blurred vision occur in severe cases.
  • Patients may complain of rapid diminution of vision if exudative RD occurs.
Treatment
  • Presence of retinopathy is an indication for urgent elective termination of pregnancy to save vision and life of the mother and adequate general care.
  • If this is done in time, it usually leads to recovery and even the detachment will resolve.
The fundus picture shows
  • Severe arteriolar spasm and constriction.
  • Macular star.
  • Flame-shaped hemorrhages.
  • Papilledema.
  • Cotton wool spots.
  • Severe exudation which may be so profuse to produce serous retinal detachment.
  • No arteriosclerosis because hypertension is not longstanding.
