Blunt ocular injury · lens and vitreous

Rosette cataract,
dislocation, haemorrhage

Concussion cataract classically has a rosette shape. The lens may dislocate or subluxate. Vitreous injury includes haemorrhage, liquefaction and posterior detachment.

Injuries to the lens

  • A · Concussion/contusion cataract: rosette-shaped cataract is the most typical appearance; usually in the posterior cortex, sometimes anterior or both.
  • B · Dislocation/subluxation of the lens.
  • C · Lens particle glaucoma.

The vitreous

  • a · Vitreous haemorrhage.
  • b · Vitreous liquefaction and posterior vitreous detachment.
A rosette cataract is the classic morphological clue to blunt lens trauma.
