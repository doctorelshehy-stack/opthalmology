Definition

Optic atrophy

The term applied to the optic disc when the optic nerve is degenerated, due to interruption of nerve fibres anywhere between the ganglion cell layer and the lateral geniculate body.

1 · Primary optic atrophy

Due to a cause outside the eyeball.

CNS diseaseOptic nerve damage due to
MS — commonest causeInjury: fracture of the base of the skull
NeurosyphilisPressure: tumours such as pituitary tumours
Ischaemia: massive haemorrhage (blood loss)

2 · Secondary optic atrophy

Due to an optic disc disease.

  • Post-papilledemic: papilledema.
  • Post-neuritic: optic neuritis.

3 · Consecutive optic atrophy

Due to a chorio-retinal disease.

  • Central retinal artery occlusion.
  • Retinitis pigmentosa.

4 · Post-glaucomatous optic atrophy

Optic nerve degeneration in the setting of glaucoma.
