Fundus · late changes

Champagne cork,
then atrophy

Long-standing papilledema produces a marked, bulbous disc; untreated, it progresses to post-papilledemic optic atrophy.

c · Long-standing papilledema

  • Marked elevation of the optic disc with a champagne cork appearance.
  • Cotton-wool spots and haemorrhages are absent.
  • Opto-ciliary shunts and corpora amylacea on the disc surface.

d · Atrophic papilledema

Post-papilledemic optic atrophy

The end-stage of untreated or prolonged papilledema, with significant visual failure.

Visual field

  • Early: enlargement of the blind spot.
  • Late: central scotoma for blue (rare).

Pupil

  • Papilledema: rounded, regular and reactive (RRR).
  • Post-papilledemic optic atrophy: sluggish or no reaction to light.
