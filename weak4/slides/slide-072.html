Lid position · Lagophthalmos Week 4 · Ophthalmology

Management

Protect the
cornea

Treatment aims at protecting the cornea: temporary lubrication and closure, then surgical narrowing of the fissure, and removal of the cause.

1 · Temporary protection

  • In mild degrees, ointment is instilled at night.
  • Closure of the lids by narrow strips of plaster.
  • A contact lens can be used when the lids cannot be brought together.

2 · Surgical treatment

Indicated when it is difficult to close the eye by the previous methods:

  1. Lateral tarsorrhaphy: reduce the width of the palpebral fissure by uniting the edges of the lids at the lateral canthus.
  2. Median tarsorrhaphy: union of the whole lid margin, sometimes indicated in cases with severe lagophthalmos.

3 · Remove the cause

Removal of the cause of lagophthalmos.

Corneal protection comes first; definitive management follows once the cause is established.
