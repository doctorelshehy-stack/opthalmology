Near response

Accommodation,
convergence, miosis

Fixation of a near object produces three linked responses. The pathway involves the visual cortex and frontal cortex before descending to the oculomotor nucleus.

01

Accommodation

Contraction of the ciliary muscle → increase in the refractive power of the lens → brings the image onto the retina.

02

Convergence

Contraction of the medial recti → brings the image onto the foveae.

03

Miosis

Constriction of the pupil → increases the depth of focus.

Pathway

  • Stimulus: blurring of the retinal image of a near object.
  • Receptors: rods and cones.
  • Afferent: retinal ganglion cell axons → optic nerve → optic tract → LGB → optic radiations → visual cortex → frontal cortex, from which fibres descend in the internal capsule to the oculomotor nuclear complex.
  • Centre: Edinger–Westphal nucleus.
  • Efferent: as the light reflex.
  • N.B.: the consensual reaction is caused by the decussation of fibres at the chiasma and the midbrain.
