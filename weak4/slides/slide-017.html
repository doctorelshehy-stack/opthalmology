Retina · Diabetic retinopathy

Treatment: laser, injections & surgery

3) Laser photocoagulation: the mainstay of treatment

Aim: to eliminate ischemic areas to decrease vasoformative substances and increase vascularization of other areas.

A. Panretinal photocoagulation (PRP)

  • Proliferative diabetic retinopathy.
  • Neovascular glaucoma with useful vision & clear media.
  • Rubeosis with clear media.

B. Laser treatment for clinically significant macular edema

  • Focal laser for focal macular edema.
  • Grid laser for diffuse macular edema.
4) Anti-VEGF

Intravitreal injection of Anti-vascular Endothelial Growth Factor (Anti- VEGF): now, it is the mainstay in treatment of diabetic macular edema.

5) Triamcinolone acetonide

Intravitreal injection of Triamcinolone acetonide (4 mg) injection: for persistent macular edema.

6) Surgical treatment

Vitrectomy & endolaser: for persistent vitreous hemorrhage and for tractional retinal detachment.
