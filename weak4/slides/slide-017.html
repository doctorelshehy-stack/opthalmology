Papillitis:
definition and symptoms

An acute, active disc swelling associated with visual loss. Visual loss is rapid and may reach perception of light; pain is characteristic.

Definition

Papillitis usually describes an acute, active, disc swelling that is associated with visual loss.

1 · Rapid progressive visual loss

Rapidly progressive visual loss, up to perception of light (PL).

2 · Pain

A dull retrobulbar ache or sharp ocular pain, which may be provoked by eye movements — especially adduction and elevation.

Why eye movement hurts: the superior rectus and medial rectus are connected to the dural sheath of the optic nerve. Movement therefore tugs on the inflamed nerve.

The rectus muscles and the optic nerve sheath share connective-tissue attachments.
