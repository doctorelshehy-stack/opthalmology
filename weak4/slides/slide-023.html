Retinopathy of prematurity: pathogenesis & stages

Pathogenesis
  • Retinal blood vessels reach nasal periphery at the 8th month of gestation and reach temporal periphery one month after delivery.
  • Retinopathy of prematurity (ROP) is a proliferative retinopathy affects pre-term infants <30ws and low birth weight <1500 gm who exposed to high oxygen.
  • High oxygen concentration leads to damage to incomplete vascularized retina.
Classification: according to severity Stage
Stage 1 Demarcation line separates vascular from avascular retina.
Stage 2 Ridge: Shunts joins arteries and veins.
Stage 3 Ridge associated with extraretinal fibrovascular proliferation.
Stage 4 Subtotal retinal detachment formation.
Stage 5 Total retinal detachment formation.
