Papilledema · Symptoms Week 4 · Ophthalmology

Clinical picture · symptoms

Raised pressure,
quiet vision

Symptoms of raised intracranial pressure dominate early. Vision may remain normal for a long time before transient obscurations or permanent failure.

1 · Symptoms of increased intracranial pressure

  • Headache.
  • Vomiting.
  • Blurring of vision.
  • Diplopia.

2 · Visual symptoms

a

May be absent for a long time

There may be no visual symptoms for a long time.

b

Transient visual loss

Later, transient attacks of loss of vision lasting for less than a minute.

c

Secondary optic atrophy

If untreated, secondary post-papilledemic optic atrophy develops with significant visual failure.
