Margin · 2 of 2

Anterior border,
grey line, white line, posterior border

Each border has a distinct surgical and anatomical significance; the grey line separates the anterior and posterior lamellae.

Anterior border

Rounded, carries lashes

  • The lashes are modified hairs.
  • Arranged in 2 or 3 rows.
  • Related to sebaceous Zeiss glands and modified sweat Moll glands.

Grey line

Surgical plane

  • Corresponds to the neurovascular bundle between orbicularis and tarsus.
  • The lid can be surgically split here into anterior and posterior lamellae.

White line

Meibomian orifices

  • The openings of the meibomian glands.
  • Behind the grey line, representing the orifices of the meibomian glands.

Posterior border

Sharp, tear-conducting

  • Sharp to help conduction of tears.
  • Placed against the globe.
