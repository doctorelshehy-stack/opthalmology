Blunt ocular injury · cornea and sclera

Abrasion, rupture
and globe integrity

Corneal abrasion is diagnosed with fluorescein. Corneal rupture and scleral rupture require urgent surgical repair and restoration of globe integrity.

a · Corneal abrasion

Diagnosis: by fluorescein test (positive).

Treatment

  • Bandage or soft, therapeutic contact lenses.
  • Antibiotic to prevent infection.

b · Corneal rupture

Urgent surgical repair should be performed.

3 · Injuries to the sclera

Scleral rupture (rupture globe): proper suturing of the scleral rupture; the globe integrity should be restored adequately.
