Hyphema · management

Rest, control pressure,
prevent rebleeding

Treatment combines general measures, cycloplegia, selective steroids, IOP control, vitamin K and, in selected cases, surgical evacuation.

1 · General measures

  • Bed rest (semi-sitting position) — at home or in hospital according to severity.
  • If not contraindicated, stop anticoagulants (e.g. aspirin products).

2 · Cycloplegics

3 · Topical steroids

If a fibrinous anterior reaction develops, to control any associated uveitis.

4 · Antiglaucoma medications

  • If IOP is > 40 mmHg at presentation.
  • Or > 30 mmHg for 2 weeks or more.

5 · Vitamin K

To prevent rebleeding.

6 · Surgical evacuation

May be indicated in some cases.
