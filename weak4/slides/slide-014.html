Retina · Diabetic retinopathy

Clinical picture: symptoms & early signs

It is classified into stages for purposes of treatment and prognosis:

Symptoms
  • Asymptomatic.
  • Gradual diminution of vision.
  • Floaters.
  • Acute loss of vision.
1) Non-proliferative diabetic retinopathy (NPDR)
  • Microaneurysms.
  • Intraretinal dot-blot hemorrhages.
  • Lipid hard exudates.
  • Retinal edema.
2) Pre-proliferative diabetic retinopathy
  • Cotton wool spots.
  • Venous beading.
  • Intraretinal microvascular abnormalities (IRMA).
