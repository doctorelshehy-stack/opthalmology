traumatic ptosis, lacerations

Eyelid injuries range from ecchymosis to complex lacerations. Traumatic ptosis may be mechanical, paralytic or neuromyogenic.

1 · Ecchymosis

Black eye.

2 · Traumatic ptosis

  • Mechanical — due to lid oedema, ecchymosis or emphysema.
  • Paralytic — due to damage of the nerve supply of the levator muscle.
  • Neuromyogenic — due to direct injury or damage of the levator muscle.

3 · Contused and lacerated wounds

Should be sutured, and systemic antibiotics are used if contamination is suspected.
