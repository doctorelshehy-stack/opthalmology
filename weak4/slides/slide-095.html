Blunt trauma · orbit

Bleeding, motility
and wall fractures

Retrobulbar haemorrhage, ophthalmoplegia and orbital wall fractures are the key orbital consequences of blunt trauma.

1 · Orbital (retrobulbar) haemorrhage

Bleeding into the orbital space behind the globe and orbital septum.

2 · Ophthalmoplegia

Limitation of ocular motility.

3 · Orbital wall fractures

The floor, then the medial wall, is commonly fractured after blunt injuries.

Orbital compartment syndrome from retrobulbar haemorrhage is an emergency: bleeding behind the septum raises orbital pressure and can threaten the optic nerve.
