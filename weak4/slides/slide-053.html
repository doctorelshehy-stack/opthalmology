Optic neuritis · Definition Week 4 · Ophthalmology

Definition

Optic neuritis

An inflammatory, infective or demyelinating process affecting the optic nerve.

Ophthalmoscopic classification

a

Papillitis

Disc swelling with visual loss.

b

Retrobulbar neuritis

Inflammation behind the globe.

c

Neuro-retinitis

Optic nerve and retina involved together.

The ophthalmoscopic classification describes where the inflammation is seen; the etiological classification describes why it occurred.

At a glance

“An inflammatory, infective or demyelinating process affecting the optic nerve.”

Two classifications

  • Ophthalmoscopic: papillitis, retrobulbar neuritis, neuro-retinitis.
  • Etiological: demyelinating, para-infectious, infectious, autoimmune.
