Chalazion

A chronic
lipo-granuloma

A localised chronic inflammatory lipo-granuloma of a meibomian gland, caused by retained secretions after duct obstruction.

Definition

A localised chronic inflammatory lipo-granuloma of a meibomian gland.

Etiology · unknown, but may be

  • A granuloma due to irritation of the meibomian gland by retained secretions after obstruction of the duct, secondary to fibrosis or desquamation of the duct epithelium (vitamin A deficiency).
  • Low-grade bacterial infection.
  • Residual hordeolum internum.

Clinical picture

Symptoms

  • Painless swelling under the skin of the eyelid.
  • Pain occurs if it becomes infected (acute chalazion).

Signs

  • A swelling which is localised, firm, painless, not tender, with the skin freely mobile over it.
  • The palpebral conjunctiva is red or bluish-grey over it.
