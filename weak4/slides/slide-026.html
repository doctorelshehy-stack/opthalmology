Papilledema · DDx and treatment Week 4 · Ophthalmology

Differential and management

Exclude the
mimics

Pseudo-papilledema, papillitis and other causes of disc oedema must be distinguished before treatment is directed at the primary cause.

Differential diagnosis

  1. Pseudo-papilledema: disc elevation with no oedema of the optic nerve fibres — e.g. high hypermetropia.
  2. Papillitis.
  3. Other causes of optic disc oedema.

Treatment

  1. Treatment of the primary cause — e.g. tumour, abscess, haemorrhage, etc.
  2. Optic nerve decompression (fenestration) in severely affected visual functions.
The disc appearance alone does not establish the cause. Systemic evaluation is required.
