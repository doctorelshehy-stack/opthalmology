Blunt ocular injury · ciliary body

Accommodation,
angle recession, cyclodialysis

Ciliary body injury can alter accommodation, tear the angle and produce hypotony when the ciliary body is torn from the scleral spur.

Effects

  1. Spasm of accommodation — with temporary myopia.
  2. Paralysis of accommodation — with blurring of near vision.
  3. Ciliary body shutdown.
  4. Angle recession — a tear within the ciliary body itself. It results in hyphema and angle-recession glaucoma.
  5. Cyclodialysis — the ciliary body is torn from its attachment at the scleral spur. It should be suspected when IOP remains low after ocular trauma.

Two key concepts

Angle recession: a tear within the ciliary body → hyphema and angle-recession glaucoma.

Cyclodialysis: ciliary body torn from the scleral spur → suspect when IOP remains low after trauma.

The two are distinct: angle recession is a tear within the ciliary body; cyclodialysis is a disinsertion from the scleral spur.
