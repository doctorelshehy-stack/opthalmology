Diabetic retinopathy: risk factors & pathogenesis

Diabetic retinopathy (DR) is the commonest cause of blindness in working population. It causes of 10% of new cases of blindness /year.
Risk factors
  • Young age and long duration.
  • Poor control of diabetes.
  • Coexisting hypertension.
  • High blood-lipids level.
  • Pregnancy.
Pathogenesis: DR affects micro-vessels
  • Microvascular leakage:
    • Decrease in the number of pericytes leads to macroaneurysm and breakdown of blood-retina barrier.
    • This leads hemorrhages, edema and lipid exudates.
  • Microvascular occlusion:
    • Progressive thickening of capillary basement membrane and aggregation and stickiness of platelets and RBCs changes.
    • This leads to occlusion of vascular capillary, ischemia, release of vaso-formative substance and new vessels formation.
