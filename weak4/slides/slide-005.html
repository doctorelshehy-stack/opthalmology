Blood supply of the retina

Arterial supply
  • The inner five layers supplied by central retinal artery, branch of ophthalmic artery.
  • The outer five layers are avascular and supplied by diffusion from choroid.
  • In 10-20% of normal population there is cilioretinal artery.
  • It is a branch of posterior ciliary arteries.
  • It supplies the macula.
Venous drainage
  • The retina is drained by central retinal vein, a tributary of ophthalmic vein which drains in the cavernous sinus.
