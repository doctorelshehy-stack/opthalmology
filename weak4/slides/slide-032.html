Branch retinal artery occlusion & investigations

2) Branch Retinal Artery Occlusion
  • Branch retinal artery is most frequently caused by embolism & occasionally by periarteritis.
  • Symptom: sudden painless loss of sector of visual field.
  • White retina corresponding to the area of distribution of the blocked arteriole.
  • Narrowing of blood vessels in the distribution of blocked arteriole.
  • Emboli may be seen.
Investigations of retinal artery occlusion
  1. Fluorescein angiography: to confirm diagnosis.
  2. Cardiovascular workup.
  3. Blood pressure, blood picture, ESR and lipid profile.
  4. Echocardiogram and non-invasive carotid Doppler ultrasound.
