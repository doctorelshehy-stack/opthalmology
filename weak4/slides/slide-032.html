Gross anatomy

Two movable
muco-cutaneous folds

The upper lid covers 1–2 mm of the upper cornea; the lower lid sits just below the cornea. The elliptical opening is the palpebral fissure.

  • The eyelids are two movable muco-cutaneous folds.
  • The upper eyelid covers 1–2 mm of the upper part of the cornea.
  • The lower eyelid is just below the cornea.
  • The elliptical space when the eyelids are opened is the palpebral fissure; its angles are the canthi.
  • The medial canthus is rounded and surrounds the tear lake (lacus lacrimalis), which contains the caruncle and plica semilunaris.
  • In the upper eyelid, a transverse crease lies 5–10 mm above the lid margin and represents the insertion of the levator aponeurosis.

The upper lid margin rests 1–2 mm over the cornea.
