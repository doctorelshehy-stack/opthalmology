Retrobulbar neuritis

Retrobulbar
neuritis

Inflammation of the optic nerve behind the globe: acute unilateral visual loss, pain on movement and a normal fundus.

Definition

Inflammation behind the globe

Inflammation of the optic nerve behind the globe.

Clinical features

  1. Acute unilateral visual loss in a patient 20–40 years old.
  2. Ocular pain which is exacerbated by ocular movements.
  3. RAPD.
  4. Normal fundus, unless there is papillitis.
Fate and treatment are similar to those of papillitis.

The disc may appear normal because the inflammation is posterior to the globe.
