C · Conjunctival laceration

  • A complete slit-lamp examination should be done to rule out an occult scleral laceration or retained foreign body.
  • Small: conservative — antibiotic drops/ointment.
  • Large (more than 6 mm): suturing.
Subconjunctival haemorrhage alone is usually benign, but it can mask underlying scleral damage — examine the full anterior segment.
