Retina · Retinal vascular disorders

Hypertensive retinopathy: leakage, treatment, N.B.

Signs 2) Vascular leakage
  • Flame-shaped hemorrhages.
  • Retinal edema.
  • Hard exudates.
  • Papilledema and exudative retinal detachment is the hallmark of malignant hypertension.
Treatment: Control of systemic hypertension.
N.B.: Arteriosclerosis and hypertensive changes are commonly associated together.
N.B.: Pure form of hypertensive retinopathy (arteriolar narrowing and vascular leakage) is commonly seen in young individuals in cases of toxemia of pregnancy, pheochromocytoma and malignant hypertension in patients with renal failure.
