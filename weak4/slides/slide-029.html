Optic atrophy · Clinical Week 4 · Ophthalmology

Clinical picture

Visual failure and
a pale disc

Visual failure may progress to blindness; field defects are concentric contraction and scotoma. The fundus picture is summarised in Table 2 of the source.

Symptoms

  1. Visual symptoms: visual failure (till blindness); field defects — concentric contraction and scotoma.
  2. Symptoms of the causative disease.

Signs

  1. Fundus picture: Table 2.
  2. Field of vision: concentric contraction (with tubular vision); scotoma.
  3. Pupil: as in post-papilledemic optic atrophy.
Concentric contraction may progress to tubular vision.
Post-papilledemic atrophy leaves the pupil sluggish or unreactive to light.
