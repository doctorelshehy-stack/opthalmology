Lid glands · Hordeolum Week 4 · Ophthalmology

Definition

Hordeolum

A localised acute infection arising within the glands at the eyelid margin. There are two types: externum (stye) and internum (infected chalazion).

01

Hordeolum externum

Also called a stye. Acute suppurative inflammation of the Zeiss gland.

02

Hordeolum internum

Also called an infected chalazion. Acute suppurative inflammation of the meibomian gland.

Both are acute infections of lid glands. The externum points at the lash root; the internum points on the conjunctival side and is more painful.
