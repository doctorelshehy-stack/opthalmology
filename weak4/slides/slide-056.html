Papillitis:
the signs

Disc swelling, vascular congestion and haemorrhage, a relative afferent pupillary defect and a central scotoma for red and green.

1 · The fundus — disc

Elevated, hyperaemic disc

  • Elevated — not more than 3 dioptres.
  • Hyperaemic, with a filled cup.

1 · The fundus — vessels

Dilated, congested veins

  • Retinal vessels: dilated and congested veins.
  • Splinter haemorrhage on the disc.

2 · The pupil

RAPD on the affected side

Pupils are equal on both sides, with a relative afferent pupillary defect on the affected side.

3 · Field changes

Central scotoma

A central scotoma for red and green.
