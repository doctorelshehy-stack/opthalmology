Entropion · type 4

Age, laxity
and override

Age-related degeneration of elastic tissue produces horizontal laxity and loss of back support; preseptal orbicularis overrides the pretarsal part.

Pathogenesis

  • More common in the lower eyelid of older individuals, due to age-related degeneration of elastic tissue within the eyelid, leading to horizontal laxity with lack of back support.
  • Contributing factors: overriding of the pretarsal by the preseptal orbicularis oculi, and vertical eyelid instability due to weak or disinserted lower eyelid retractors.

Treatment

Mainly surgical.

The operation must address horizontal laxity and re-establish the lower lid retractor support, not merely remove lashes.
