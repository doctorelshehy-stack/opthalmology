Aims

The main aim of treatment of severe burns is to reduce inflammation, promote epithelial regeneration and prevent corneal ulceration.

  1. Topical steroid, antibiotic and cycloplegic eye drops.
  2. Tetracyclines are effective collagenase inhibitors that reduce ulceration — either topically (tetracycline ointment) or systemically (doxycycline 100 mg twice daily).
  3. IOP should be monitored, with treatment if necessary. Oral acetazolamide (Cidamex®) is recommended to avoid adding a further drop.
  4. Treatment of the complications.

Why each step matters

  • Steroid: controls inflammation.
  • Antibiotic: prevents secondary infection of the devitalised surface.
  • Cycloplegic: manages ciliary spasm and associated anterior uveitis.
  • Tetracycline/doxycycline: inhibits collagenase and reduces corneal melting.
  • IOP control: protects the optic nerve and avoids additional topical drop burden.
