Etiology

Causes of
optic neuritis

Demyelinating disease is the most common cause. Para-infectious, infectious and autoimmune causes complete the classification.

CategoryCause
DemyelinatingThe most common cause.
Para-infectiousFollows viral infection or immunisation.
InfectiousMay be sinus-related or associated with syphilis, Lyme disease, cat scratch fever, etc.
AutoimmuneAssociated with systemic autoimmune diseases.

N.B. · Demyelination

A pathological process in which normally myelinated nerve fibres lose their myelin layer, causing disruption of nervous conduction within white-matter tracts.

Demyelinating diseases affecting the eye include multiple sclerosis and Devic disease.
