Papilledema · Fundus Week 4 · Ophthalmology

Fundus · early and established

From blurred edges
to flame haemorrhage

Early papilledema is subtle; established papilledema produces marked elevation, venous engorgement, haemorrhages and exudates.

a · Early papilledema

  • Blurred optic disc edges.
  • Disc hyperaemia and mild elevation.
  • Loss of previous spontaneous venous pulsations.

b · Established papilledema

  • Severe hyperaemia and elevation of the optic disc up to 9 D (1 mm swelling = 3 D).
  • Partial or complete obliteration of the physiological cup.
  • Flame-shaped haemorrhage and cotton-wool exudates on or near the disc.
  • Engorged, dilated and tortuous retinal veins.
  • Retinal changes: macular oedema or star, and retinal stress lines.
1 mm of disc swelling ≈ 3 dioptres. This relationship is used when grading the elevation clinically.
