Retina · Retinitis pigmentosa Week 4 · Ophthalmology

Definition

Retinitis
pigmentosa

Better termed pigmentary dystrophy because there is no inflammation. It is bilateral and may be isolated or occur with other systemic disease.

  • It is a bilateral disorder that may occur isolated or with other systemic disease.
  • It may be sporadic or inherited as an autosomal dominant, autosomal recessive or X-linked trait.
  • The name is historical: the disease is a pigmentary dystrophy, not an inflammatory retinitis.
Autosomal dominant Autosomal recessive X-linked Sporadic

Prevalence

1/5000 of the population

Bilateral, inherited and progressive — with no inflammation in the name or the pathology.
