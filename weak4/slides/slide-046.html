Optic nerve
and chiasm

The optic nerve carries fibres from the lamina cribrosa to the chiasm. At the chiasm, nasal fibres cross to the opposite optic tract.

1 · The optic nerve

The second cranial nerve. It runs from the lamina cribrosa to the optic chiasm, with an average length of 4 cm.

2 · The optic chiasm

The central binding formed by the two optic nerves anteriorly and the two optic tracts posteriorly.

Here, the nasal fibres of each retina cross to pass in the optic tract of the opposite side.

Optic nerve → chiasm. Nasal retinal fibres decussate; temporal fibres remain ipsilateral.
