Hemorrhage

The appearance of retinal hemorrhage depends in part on the anatomic location of the blood.

  1. Hemorrhages in the nerve fiber layer are flame shaped.
  2. Hemorrhages in the deeper retinal layers have a dot and blot appearance.
  3. Sub-hyaloid location produces a boat shaped hemorrhage.
Edema
  • Result from damage to the vessel wall that causes leakage of serum and retinal thickening.
  • Edema in the macula appears as macular star.
Hard exudates
  • Result from chronic retinal edema which leads to lipid deposition as a yellowish lesion recognized clinically as a hard exudate.
