Physiology

Three orders
of neurons

Like all afferent sensory systems, the visual pathway is described as having three orders of neurons, excluding the photoreceptors.

01

First order

The bipolar cells.

02

Second order

The retinal ganglion cells and their axons in the nerve fibre layer and the optic nerve, up to the lateral geniculate body.

03

Third order

Extends from the lateral geniculate body to the visual cortex along the optic radiation.

Photoreceptors → Bipolar cells → Ganglion cells → LGB → Visual cortex
The three orders exclude the photoreceptors, which are the sensory receptors rather than a neuronal order in this scheme.
