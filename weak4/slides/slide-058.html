Acquired mal-directed lashes

Cicatricial lid
disease at the margin

Any cicatrizing disease affecting the lid margin can misdirect lashes. Trachoma is the commonest cause in Egypt.

Causes

Any cicatrizing disease affecting the lid margin:

  1. Trachoma — the commonest cause in Egypt.
  2. Ulcerative blepharitis.
  3. Trauma and chemical burns.
  4. Cicatrizing diseases: ocular cicatricial pemphigoid and Stevens–Johnson disease.

Symptoms

Foreign-body sensation, pain, lacrimation, photophobia and conjunctival hyperaemia.

Signs

  • Blepharospasm.
  • Misdirected lashes with or without entropion.
  • Conjunctival hyperaemia with or without discharge.
  • Corneal vascularisation in long-standing cases, due to prolonged corneal irritation.
