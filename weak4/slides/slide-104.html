Blunt ocular injury · optic nerve and IOP

Avulsion, neuropathy,
hypotony and glaucoma

Optic nerve injury may be laceration or avulsion. Intraocular pressure may fall in hypotony or rise in secondary glaucoma through four mechanisms.

11 · Optic nerve injuries

  • a · Laceration or avulsion.
  • b · Optic neuropathy.

12 · Intraocular pressure

a · Hypotony — causes

  • Cyclitis with loss of function.
  • Rupture globe with perforation of the outer coat.

b · Secondary glaucoma

By four different mechanisms.

A low IOP after trauma should prompt a search for cyclodialysis or globe rupture; a high IOP may reflect angle recession, hyphema, lens particles or another secondary mechanism.
