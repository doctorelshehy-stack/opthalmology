Blunt ocular injury · choroid and retina

Rupture, breaks,
commotio retinae

Choroidal haemorrhage and rupture, retinal breaks and dialysis, traumatic macular hole and commotio retinae are the posterior segment consequences.

The choroid

  • a · Choroidal haemorrhage.
  • b · Choroidal rupture.

Injuries to the retina

  • a · Retinal breaks: occur particularly at the retinal periphery, more commonly in high-myopic eyes; usually followed by retinal detachment.
  • b · Traumatic macular hole.
  • c · Commotio retinae (Berlin's oedema): a contusion injury with transient white oedema of the deep sensory retina following ocular trauma; typically involves the retina opposite the side of an anterior-segment impact. Usually asymptomatic unless involving the macula.
  • d · Retinal dialysis: disinsertion of the peripheral retina from its attachment at the ora serrata.
