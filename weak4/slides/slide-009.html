Retina · Vascular disorders

Retinal vascular diseases: changes in the blood vessels

Retinal vessels are affected by many systemic and local conditions that produce pathological changes in the retina not specific for any disease.

Changes in the blood vessels

Sclerosis

Thickening of the walls of retinal arterioles develops in arteriosclerosis.

Spasm of blood vessels

Occurs in cases with malignant hypertension.

Microaneurysms

Are fusiform or saccular outpouchings of capillaries commonly seen in diabetic retinopathy.

Leakage from microcirculation: hemorrhage, edema and hard exudates. Occlusion of microcirculation: cotton-wool spots and neovascularization.
