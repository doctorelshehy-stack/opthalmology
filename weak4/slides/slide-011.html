Retina · Vascular disorders

Occlusion of microcirculation

Cotton-wool spots

Cotton-wool spots or soft exudates which are microinfarctions of nerve fiber layer usually secondary to occlusion of retinal arteriole (ischemic infarction).

Neovascularization
  • Growth of new vessels on the vitreous side of the retina.
  • It may occur in conditions characterized by retinal hypoxia.
  • These abnormal vessels leak and bleed.
