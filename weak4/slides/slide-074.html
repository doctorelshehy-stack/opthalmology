Abnormal responses · C and D

Light fails,
near works

Light–near dissociation means an absent or sluggish light reflex with a normal near response. Argyll Robertson and Adie pupils are the classic examples.

C · Light–near dissociation

The light reflex is absent or sluggish, but the near response is normal.

1 · Argyll Robertson pupil

Causes: neurosyphilis.

  • Bilateral, asymmetrical, small and irregular pupils.
  • The pupils are difficult to dilate.

2 · Adie (tonic) pupil

Causes: viral illness.

  • Large regular pupil (except when old).
  • Responds slowly in the near reflex, followed by slow redilatation.

D · Hutchinson pupil

Causes: traumatic subdural haemorrhage, where consciousness is often disturbed.
