Retina · Retinal vascular disorders

Grading of arteriosclerosis

Grade Fundus changes
Grade 1 Subtle broadening of the arteriolar light reflex, mild generalized arteriolar attenuation and vein concealment.
Grade 2 Obvious broadening of the arteriolar light reflex associated with deflection of veins at arteriovenous crossings.
Grade 3 Copper-wiring of arterioles, banking of veins distal to arteriovenous crossings, tapering of veins on either side of the crossings associated with right angled deflection of veins.
Grade 4 Silver-wiring of arterioles associated with grade 3 changes.
