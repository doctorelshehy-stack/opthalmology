Papillitis · 3 of 3

Fate, differential
and treatment

Recovery usually occurs within weeks, but post-neuritic atrophy and recurrence are possible. Exclude other causes of disc swelling and compressive lesions.

Fate

  • Recovery within 1–4 weeks, but may take longer.
  • Secondary (post-neuritic) optic atrophy.
  • Recurrence.

Differential diagnosis

  • Other causes of disc swelling — see papilledema.
  • Other optic nerve lesions, e.g. a compressive neuropathy.

Treatment

  1. Treatment of the cause.
  2. Steroid therapy.
  3. Neurological consultation — e.g. search for multiple sclerosis.
Papillitis is a diagnosis of the disc appearance and visual loss. The underlying cause still needs to be identified — demyelination is the most common.
