Physical injuries

Thermal, electrical
and radiational

Thermal burns usually affect the lids because the cornea and conjunctiva are protected by reflex lid closure. Electrical injury most commonly causes cataract.

1 · Thermal burns

  • Caused by thermal trauma by steam, hot water and molten metals.
  • Usually involves the eyelids in the form of a burn of the skin; it rarely affects the cornea and conjunctiva, as they are protected by reflex closure of the eyelids.

2 · Electric burns

  • Caused by direct electric shock.
  • The most common complication is cataract formation.

3 · Radiational burns

Radiational injury to the eye and adnexa.
