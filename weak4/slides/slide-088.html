Classification

Non-mechanical
and mechanical

The eyeball is protected by the bony orbit, eyelids and the blinking reflex. Injuries are classified into non-mechanical and mechanical groups.

1 · Non-mechanical

a · Chemical

  • Alkali.
  • Acid.

b · Physical

  • Thermal.
  • Electrical.
  • Radiational.

2 · Mechanical

  • c · Extraocular foreign body.
  • d · Non-penetrating trauma: blunt trauma, concussion, contusion.
  • e · Penetrating trauma by a sharp object.
The first distinction is whether the injury is chemical/physical or mechanical; the second is whether the globe remains intact or has been penetrated.
