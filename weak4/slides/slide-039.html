The upper lid elevator

Levator palpebrae
superioris

Arises at the orbital apex, runs forward over the superior rectus and becomes the fibrous aponeurosis that inserts into tarsus, skin and fornix.

Action

Elevates the upper eyelid.

Course

Arises at the apex of the orbit and runs forward over the superior rectus, then becomes the fibrous aponeurosis.

Origin

Lesser wing of the sphenoid bone.

Insertion

  • Main insertion on the anterior surface of tarsus.
  • Skin 2 mm below the superior palpebral furrow (eyelid crease).
  • Lateral and medial horns into the orbital margin, medial and lateral palpebral ligaments and nearby bone.
  • Upper fornix.
Supplied by the oculomotor nerve (3rd nerve); paralysis leads to ptosis.
