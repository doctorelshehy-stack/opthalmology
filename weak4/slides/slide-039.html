Retina · Retinal detachment

III. Exudative retinal detachment

Separation is by fluid derived from the choroid and accumulates in the subretinal space.

Causes
  • Choroidal tumors such as malignant melanoma.
  • Intra ocular inflammation such as Harada's disease, Coat's disease and posterior scleritis.
  • Systemic diseases such as toxemia of pregnancy and malignant hypertension.
Symptoms
  • Progressive loss of vision and visual field defect which may develop suddenly and progress rapidly, Floaters and pain.
Signs
  1. No retinal breaks, May be shallow or bullous convex shaped detachment.
  2. In bullous detachment the retina is very mobile with characteristic shifting of fluid in which the subretinal fluid moves rapidly to the most dependent position.
  3. The cause of RD such as choroidal tumor may be present.
Complications
  • Permanent visual loss due to disruption of RPE.
Treatment
  • Treatment of the cause.
