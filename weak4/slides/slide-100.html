Blunt ocular injury · iris

Miosis, mydriasis,
iritis and iridodialysis

Iridodialysis is disinsertion of the iris root, producing a D-shaped pupillary aperture; it may cause uniocular diplopia and mimic melanoma or a peripheral iridectomy.

Types of iris injury

  1. Traumatic miosis.
  2. Traumatic mydriasis.
  3. Post-traumatic iritis.
  4. Traumatic aniridia.
  5. Iridodialysis — a part of the iris is disinserted at its root, producing a D-shaped pupillary aperture through which the red reflex can be seen.

Iridodialysis · clinical picture

  • Polycoria — the appearance of multiple pupils.
  • May produce uniocular diplopia if the dialysis is within the palpebral fissure.
  • D-shaped pupil.
D.D.: malignant melanoma of the iris; peripheral iridectomy.

The disinserted iris root creates a second aperture and a D-shaped pupil.
