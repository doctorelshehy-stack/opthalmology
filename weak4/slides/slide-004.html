Retina · Retinitis pigmentosa Week 4 · Ophthalmology

Clinical picture · symptoms

What the patient
notices

Night vision fails first; peripheral vision is lost; central and colour vision are affected late in the course.

  1. Difficult night vision — night blindness (nyctalopia).
  2. Loss of peripheral vision — progressive constriction of the visual field.
  3. Late poor central vision — macular involvement occurs late.
  4. Late poor colour vision — colour discrimination is affected late.
The combination of night blindness and peripheral field loss is the characteristic symptomatic presentation; central vision is relatively preserved until late.
