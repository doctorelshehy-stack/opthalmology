Arteriosclerosis: clinical signs

Arteriosclerosis is an age-related process in which there is thickening and sclerosis of arterioles.
Clinical sign

Straight and sheathed arterioles.

Light reflex
  • Increased light reflex and loss of transparency of blood column.
  • Copper wire arterioles.
  • Silver wire arterioles.
Arteriovenous crossing changes
  • Concealment of part of vein (nicking).
  • Vertical and lateral deflection of veins (Salus's sign).
  • Banking; compression and distension of distal part of the vein (Bonnet sign).
  • Tapering of the vein on either side of crossing (Gunn's sign).
