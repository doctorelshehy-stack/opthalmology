Beyond the triad

Associated features
and management

Look for associated ocular disease. There is no definite treatment for RP itself; management is supportive and directed at complications.

Associated features

  • Posterior subcapsular cataract.
  • Myopia.
  • Open-angle glaucoma.
  • Keratoconus.
  • Posterior vitreous detachment.
  • Cystoid macular edema.

Management

  1. No definite treatment for RP is currently known.
  2. Treat associated ocular problems, such as cataract.
  3. Low vision aids to maximise remaining vision.
  4. Genetic counselling for the patient and family.
Management is therefore directed at the associated ocular problems, visual rehabilitation and genetic counselling — not at reversing the dystrophy.
