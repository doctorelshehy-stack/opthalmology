Lid position · Ptosis Week 4 · Ophthalmology

Management

Resect the levator
or suspend the lid

Congenital ptosis is timed to protect vision and development. Acquired ptosis is treated by cause. The operation depends on levator action and the amount of ptosis.

A · Congenital — age of interference

  • Mild to moderate: wait until school age (5–6 years).
  • Severe: as early as possible to avoid amblyopia.

B · Acquired — treat the cause

  1. Mechanical: remove the cause (tumour or multiple chalazia).
  2. Hysterical: psychoanalysis.
  3. Myasthenia gravis: prostigmine.
  4. Paralytic or traumatic: wait 6 months, then re-evaluate the ptosis.
  5. Senile: levator aponeurosis repair or levator resection.

Choice of operation

Depends on the amount of ptosis and levator action:

1 · If the levator has some action (fair or good): resection and advancement to strengthen the muscle.

  • Blascovic's operation — from the conjunctival side.
  • Everbusch's operation — from the skin side.

2 · If levator function is poor and ptosis is severe:

  • Frontalis suspension (sling) — Hiss operation.
