Miosis versus mydriasis · 1 of 2

Physiology
and drugs

Miosis is a pupil diameter below 2 mm; mydriasis is above 7 mm. Physiological state and drugs are the first two categories.

CategoryMiosis · pupil < 2 mmMydriasis · pupil > 7 mm
Physiological
  • Light and near reflexes.
  • During sleep.
  • 3rd stage of anaesthesia.
  • Old age / newborn.
  • Withdrawal of light.
  • Emotions (stress).
  • 2nd stage of anaesthesia.
Drugs
  • Miotics.
  • Morphine poisoning.
  • Mydriatics.
  • Datura poisoning.
The physiological categories are useful bedside clues: the pupil tells you about light, sleep, anaesthetic depth and age.
