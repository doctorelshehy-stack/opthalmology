The tract ends in the lateral geniculate body; axons then form the optic radiation and terminate in the occipital cortex.

03

The optic tract

Runs posteriorly to enter the lateral geniculate body — except the afferent pupillary fibres, which terminate in the pretectal nucleus of the midbrain.

04

The lateral geniculate body

Located at the end of the optic tract. Here the axons of retinal ganglion cells synapse with cells whose axons constitute the optic radiation.

05

The optic radiation

Passes through the most posterior part of the internal capsule, then spreads into a band running posteriorly to the occipital lobe.

06

The visual cortex

Present on the medial aspect and posterior pole of the occipital lobe.

Tract → LGB → optic radiation → occipital cortex. Pupillary fibres branch before the LGB.

Each optic tract contains the temporal fibres of the same retina and the nasal fibres of the contralateral retina.
