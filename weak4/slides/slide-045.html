Blepharitis · Etiology Week 4 · Ophthalmology

Predisposition and cause

Bad health,
bad eye, bad hygiene

Three predisposing domains are classically taught. Anterior blepharitis is either metabolic/seborrhoeic or staphylococcal.

Bad health

  • Poor immunity.
  • Seborrhoeic dermatitis.
  • Acne rosacea.

Bad eye

  • Uncorrected errors of refraction.
  • Dry eye syndromes.

Bad hygiene

Poor lid and facial hygiene.

Squamous (seborrhoeic) type

Metabolic, as part of generalised seborrhoea with scalp dandruff and seborrhoeic dermatitis.

Ulcerative type

Caused by Staphylococcus aureus infection of the eyelid margin.
