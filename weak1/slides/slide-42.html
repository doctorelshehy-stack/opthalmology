Part I · Ectropion

Mechanical and paralytic ectropion

c) Mechanical ectropion

  • Due to an increase in the weight of the lower eyelid, due to a large tumor or multiple chalazia.
  • Treatment: of the cause.

d) Paralytic ectropion

  • Due to paralysis of the orbicularis affecting the lower eyelid.
  • Common cause: lower motor neuron lesions of the facial nerve (Bell’s palsy).

Clinical picture

  • Symptoms: incomplete closure of the eyelids; lacrimation (failure of the lacrimal pump mechanism).
  • Signs: incomplete blinking, lagophthalmos, inability to elevate the eyebrow, less defined nasolabial fold, and inability to pucker the mouth.
