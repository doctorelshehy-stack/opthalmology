Part I · Lid malposition

Ectropion — definition, degrees and types

Definition: ectropion is defined as rolling out (outward rotation) of the lid margin.

Degrees of ectropion

Mild
Punctum visible
The lacrimal punctum is visible without pulling of the lid.
Moderate
Conjunctiva exposed
The tarsal conjunctiva is exposed.
Severe
Fully everted lid
A completely everted lid with exposure of the conjunctival fornix.

Etiological types

  • Congenital.
  • Acquired:
    • Cicatricial.
    • Senile (involutional).
    • Mechanical.
    • Paralytic.
