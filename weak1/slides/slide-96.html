Part III · Proptosis work-up

Clinical approach — examination

a) Full ophthalmologic examination

VA, IOP, pupil, fundus, etc.

b) Inspection

  • Confirm the proptosis: standing behind the patient and noticing the level of the two eyes on tilting the head backwards.
  • Measure the degree: distance between the corneal apex and the lateral orbital rim, using a transparent ruler or Hertel’s exophthalmometer.
  • Direction: axial in intraconal lesions, displaced in extraconal lesions.
  • Laterality: unilateral or bilateral.
  • Characteristic signs: lid lag in thyroid ophthalmopathy; pulsating proptosis in carotid-cavernous fistula.

Palpation

  • For detection of thrill in carotid-cavernous fistula.
  • For detection of erosion of orbital bones by orbital tumors.

Auscultation

  • For detection of bruit in carotid-cavernous fistula.
Measure, don’t guess Exophthalmometry gives an objective, comparable value; the normal upper limit is about 2–3 mm of inter-eye asymmetry.
