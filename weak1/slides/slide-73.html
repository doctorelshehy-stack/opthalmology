Part II · Dacryocystitis treatment

Treatment — congenital dacryocystitis

Aim: restore communication between the lacrimal passages and the nose, and treat any kind of infection.

Stepwise approach in congenital dacryocystitis:

  1. First 9–12 months of age: medical treatment in the form of topical antibiotics and digital massage (by the mother using her little finger) on the sac, then in a downward direction toward the nose, to push the secretions and open Hasner’s valve.
  2. No improvement after the age of 1 year: probing is indicated. Usually probing once cures most of the cases, yet it could be repeated once more.
  3. If the second probing fails too: silastic intubation of the lacrimal passages is performed.
  4. If intubation fails: dacryocystorhinostomy is indicated.
