Part II · Lacrimal gland disease

Diseases of the lacrimal gland

  • 1) Dacryoadenitis — inflammation of the lacrimal gland.
  • 2) Miscellaneous: as Mikulicz’s syndrome — symmetrical elastic and non-tender swelling of the lacrimal and salivary glands, probably lymphomas of unknown etiology.
  • 3) Lacrimal gland tumors: adenoma, simple mixed or malignant lacrimal gland tumors.
  • 4) Atrophy of the lacrimal gland (keratoconjunctivitis sicca or Sjogren’s syndrome).

Clinical picture of lacrimal swelling

  • Up and temporal lid swelling (palpable mass below the outer lateral orbital margin) with S-shaped deformity and mechanical ptosis.
  • Possibly proptosis, down and in push of the globe with consequent up and temporal limitation of ocular movement.
Shape matters An S-shaped upper lid deformity with a firm mass in the lacrimal fossa should be treated as a lacrimal gland tumour until proved otherwise.
