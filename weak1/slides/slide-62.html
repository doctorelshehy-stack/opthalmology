Part II · Drainage system

Components of the drainage pathway

1) The 2 lacrimal puncti

  • Upper and lower openings located 6 mm from the inner canthus, at the posterior edge of the lid margin, at the junction between the lacrimal and the ciliary parts of the lid margin.
  • Each punctum is situated over a slight elevation, the lacrimal papilla.

2) The 2 canaliculi

  • Fine tubes carrying tears from the puncta to the lacrimal sac.
  • Each is formed of a vertical (2 mm) part and a horizontal (8 mm) part, that either open separately or unite into a common canal before entering the lateral wall of the lacrimal sac.

3) The lacrimal sac

  • Lies in the lacrimal fossa in the medial wall of the orbit.
  • Horner’s muscle (pars lacrimalis) — a part of the orbicularis oculi that surrounds the lacrimal sac.

4) Nasolacrimal duct

  • Its end is guarded by Hasner’s valve — a one-way valve allowing tears down but not air up.

Tear drainage

  • Evaporation: 25% of tears.
  • Excretion: passive — gravity and capillarity; active — pump mechanism of the lacrimal sac.
