Part II · Dacryocystitis

Chronic dacryocystitis — etiology

Definition

Chronic inflammation of the lacrimal sac secondary to obstruction of the nasolacrimal duct.

Predisposing factors

  • Common in females at the menopausal age, as they have a long narrow duct with hypertrophied mucosa under the influence of hormonal disturbance.

Causative organisms

  • Pneumococci in 80%.
  • Staphylococci, streptococcus, trachoma or fungi.
  • Rarely T.B. and syphilis.

Nasolacrimal duct obstruction

  • In the lumen: concretions started as a foreign body with accumulated epithelial debris.
  • In the wall: tumor, edema, congestion or post-inflammatory stricture.
  • Outside the wall: fracture of the bony canal, maxillary sinus tumors.

Obstruction of the lower end of the NLD

  • Nasal polypi, marked deviated septum or hypertrophied inferior turbinate, Hasner’s valve obstruction.
