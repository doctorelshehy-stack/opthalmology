Part I · Blepharitis

Predisposing factors and etiology

Predisposing factors

  • Bad health: poor immunity, seborrheic dermatitis and acne rosacea.
  • Bad eye: uncorrected errors of refraction and dry eye syndromes.
  • Bad hygiene.
Memory aid bad health · bad eye · bad hygiene.

Etiology of anterior blepharitis

  • In the squamous type the condition is metabolic, as a part of generalized seborrhea with scalp dandruff and seborrheic dermatitis.
  • In the ulcerative type it is caused by Staph. aureus infection of the eyelid margin.
