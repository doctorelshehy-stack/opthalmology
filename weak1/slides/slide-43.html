Part I · Ectropion

Treatment of paralytic ectropion

Temporary measures

  • Help nerve regeneration by vitamin B complex, vasodilator, cortisone and non-steroidal anti-inflammatory drugs.
  • Help the muscle by massage and electric stimulation.
  • Protect the cornea by artificial tears during the day and ointment during sleep, as well as strapping of the lids.

If the paralysis is permanent

  • Tarsorrhaphy: upper and lower lid margins stitched together to prevent exposure keratopathy.
  • Canthoplasty: lateral and medial shortening of the canthal tendon.
  • Subcutaneous fascia lata sling: fixed to the medial canthal tendon, passed subcutaneously below the lower lid and fixed to the temporalis fascia.
  • Lateral tarsal strip: lateral part of the tarsus fixed to the lateral canthal tendon.
  • Silicon or fascia lata sling encircling the eyelid.
