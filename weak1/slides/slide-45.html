Part I · Lagophthalmos

Etiology of lagophthalmos

General causes

  • Extreme debility, unconsciousness and general anesthesia.

Local — physiological

  • Occurs during sleep (especially in children) due to lack of tone of the orbicularis.

Local — pathological

Lesions in the lid:

  • Severe ectropion.
  • Structural loss (coloboma).
  • Postoperative (overcorrection of ptosis or entropion).
  • Spastic lid retraction, e.g. thyroid ophthalmopathy.
  • Lower motor neuron facial palsy.

Lesions in the globe:

  • Proptosis, anterior staphyloma, buphthalmos … etc.
