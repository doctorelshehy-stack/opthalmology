Part II · Epiphora work-up

Investigation of a case of epiphora

History — exclude lacrimation

  • Bilateral watering: usually due to lacrimation.
  • Unilateral watering: usually due to epiphora.

Examination

  • Eyelids: exclude the presence of ectropion and trichiasis.
  • Lacrimal sac: swelling and dacryocystitis.
  • Nose: polypi, deviated septum.

Regurgitation test

  • Press on the lacrimal sac against the lacrimal bone.
  • +Ve regurge: reflux of pus or tears from the puncta in obstruction of the nasolacrimal duct — a definite proof of obstruction.
  • –Ve regurge: no reflux, with patent lacrimal passages.
First bedside step Regurgitation testing separates sac/duct obstruction from purely reflex watering before any dye is used.
