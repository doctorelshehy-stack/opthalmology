Part I · Physiology

Functions of the eyelid

Protection
Protection of the globe from external injury.
Tear distribution
Help tear distribution by blinking, so help in proper wetting of the eye.
Tear drainage
Help tear drainage through the lacrimal pump.
Aqueous drainage
Help aqueous drainage through ocular massage.
Applied anatomy Failure of any single function explains most lid complaints: exposure (no closure), dry eye (no blinking), epiphora (no pump) and raised intraocular pressure (no massage).
