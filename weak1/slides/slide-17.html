Blepharitis — definition and types

It is chronic inflammation of the eyelid margin. There are three main types:

1) Anterior blepharitis

Inflammation affects the skin around the base of the eyelashes. Usually subdivided further into staphylococcal and seborrheic variants.

2) Posterior blepharitis

Inflammation affects the meibomian glands, causing meibomian gland dysfunction.

3) Mixed blepharitis

A combination of both anterior and posterior blepharitis.
