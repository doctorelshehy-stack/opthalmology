Part I · Lid margin

The four zones of the ciliary part

Anterior border The grey line The white line Posterior border
Rounded and carries the eyelashes. The lashes are modified hairs arranged in 2 or 3 rows, related to Zeiss’ sebaceous glands and Moll’s modified sweat glands. Corresponding to the neurovascular bundle between the orbicularis and the tarsus. The lid can be surgically split in the grey line into anterior and posterior lamellae. The openings of the meibomian glands; behind the grey line, representing the orifices of the meibomian glands. Sharp, to help conduction of tears; placed against the globe.
N.B. The grey line is the surgical landmark of the lid margin — splitting here separates the muscle–skin lamella from the tarsal–conjunctival lamella.
