Part I · Ectropion

Congenital and cicatricial ectropion

1) Congenital ectropion

  • Rare, dated since birth.
  • Usually associated with other lid anomalies, as in blepharophimosis syndrome.

2) Cicatricial ectropion — causes

Caused by a scar in the skin, as in:

  • Extensive heat or chemical burns of the face.
  • Skin disease: eczema, malignant tumors, scleroderma.
  • Postoperative scars or lacerated wounds of the eyelid.

Treatment of cicatricial ectropion

  • Protect the cornea from exposure until the deformity is corrected surgically.
  • Small scar: V-Y operation.
  • Large scar: Z-plasty, or removal of the scar tissue followed by eyelid grafting.
