Part II · Epiphora work-up

Imaging and ENT examination

Imaging techniques

  • Dacryocystography: visualization of the anatomic details of the lacrimal drainage system using contrast material.
  • Dacryoscintigraphy: more sensitive and less invasive method of lacrimal system imaging, especially in diagnosing functional lacrimal duct obstruction.
  • Computed tomography (CT) scan.

E.N.T. examination is important

  • To discover obstruction at the outlet.
  • To examine the state of the nasal mucosa.
Sequence of work-up history (unilateral vs bilateral) → lids, sac and nose examination → regurgitation test → Jones dye tests → imaging → ENT referral.
