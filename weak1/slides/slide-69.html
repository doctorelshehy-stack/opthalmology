Part II · Dacryocystitis

Acute dacryocystitis

Definition

Acute suppurative inflammation of the lacrimal sac.

Etiology

  • Predisposed by nasolacrimal duct obstruction or improper probing.
  • Causative organisms: pneumococci, staphylococci and streptococci.

Clinical picture

  • Symptoms: severe pain over the lacrimal sac — dull aching in the diffuse stage, becoming throbbing once pus is formed; epiphora; fever and headache.
  • Signs: tender swelling of the lacrimal sac with all signs of inflammation (redness, hotness, pain and swelling); abscess formation with fluctuation.
