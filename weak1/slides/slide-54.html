Part I · Ptosis treatment

Choice of operation

Depends on the amount of ptosis and the levator action.

1) Levator has some action (fair or good)

Resection and advancement is done to strengthen the muscle:

  • Blascovic’s operation — from the conjunctival side.
  • Everbusch’s operation — from the skin side.

2) Levator function is poor & severe ptosis

Frontalis suspension (sling) — Hiss operation.

The lid is suspended from the frontalis muscle, which replaces the paralysed levator.

Contraindications to ptosis surgery

  • Recent complete 3rd nerve palsy, because this patient has paralytic squint without diplopia (the ptosed lid covers the squinting eye); if the ptosis is corrected before the squint, diplopia will occur — so correct the squint 1st.
  • Ptosis associated with neuroparalytic keratitis or absent Bell’s phenomenon.
  • Myasthenic ptosis (medical).
  • Corneal anesthesia — to avoid exposure keratitis, as the ptosis protects the cornea.
