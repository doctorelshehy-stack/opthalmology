Part I · Blepharitis

Treatment of blepharitis

General

  • Treatment of the predisposing factors as well as treatment of associated conditions.

Local — lid hygiene

  • Warm compresses.
  • Eyelid massage.
  • Removal of scales using diluted solution of baby shampoo.

Local — medication

  • Local antibiotic ointment.
Practical sequence 1. Correct refraction and treat dry eye. 2. Warm compress + massage to express the meibomian glands. 3. Mechanical cleaning of the lashes. 4. Topical antibiotic for the ulcerative type.
