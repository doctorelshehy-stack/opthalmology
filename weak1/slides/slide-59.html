Part II · Tear film

The pre-corneal tear film

1) Lipid
Outer layer — secreted by the Meibomian glands and accessory Zeis glands. Prevents rapid tear evaporation, prevents overflow over the cheek and lubricates eyelid movement.
2) Aqueous
Middle layer — secreted by the lacrimal gland (main & accessory). Forms the main bulk of tears, cleans and flushes cellular debris and foreign bodies, and supplies oxygen and antibacterial lysozymes to the corneal epithelium.
3) Mucin
Inner layer — secreted by the goblet cells; covers the hydrophobic corneal epithelium with a mucous hydrophilic surface for the aqueous layer to spread on.
