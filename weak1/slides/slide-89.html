Part III · Thyroid eye disease

Thyroid Eye Disease (TED)

Definition

  • It is the ocular and orbital changes resulting from thyroid gland dysfunction.
  • It is the commonest cause for unilateral and bilateral proptosis.
  • It may occur in hyper-, hypo- or apparently euthyroid patients.

Etiology and pathogenesis

  • The clinical picture results from immunogenic deposition of abnormal mucopolysaccharides and collagen in the orbit and extraocular muscles.
  • Together with lymphocytic infiltration of these tissues.
  • Followed by fibrosis of the extraocular muscles.
Sequence immune deposition → lymphocytic infiltration → muscle swelling → fibrosis → restrictive myopathy.
