Part II · Dacryocystitis treatment

Treatment in adults

  1. Treatment of the cause of obstruction, e.g. relief of congestion or removal of a nasal polyp.
  2. Dacryocystorhinostomy is indicated and usually curative.
    • Principle: the lacrimal sac is connected to the nasal mucosa of the middle meatus after removal of the bone of the lacrimal fossa lying between them, creating a fistula to drain tears to the nose.
  3. Dacryocystectomy is only indicated if there is T.B. of the sac, neoplasm of the sac, atrophy or fibrosis of the sac, or an associated hypopyon corneal ulcer.
Principle of DCR Create a new pathway from the sac into the middle meatus, bypassing the obstructed nasolacrimal duct.
N.B. Dacryocystectomy (removal of the sac) is reserved for a destroyed sac or a sac tumour — it removes the septic focus but does not restore tear drainage.
