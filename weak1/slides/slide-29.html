Part I · Eyelash abnormalities

Distichiasis

Definition

An abnormality (usually congenital) in which an extra row of lashes arises from the lid margin behind the grey line, in the place of the Meibomian gland orifices.

Landmark Distichiasis = lashes from the white line (meibomian orifices); trichiasis = normal lashes misdirected backwards.

Treatment

  • Asymptomatic cases: no treatment.
  • Complicated cases: destruction of the ectopic lashes either by:
    • Direct removal.
    • Cryotherapy on the anterior surface of the tarsus after splitting the anterior from the posterior lamellae at the grey line.
