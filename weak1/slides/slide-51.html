Part I · Ptosis assessment

1) Determine the amount of ptosis

  • Measurement of the Margin to Reflex Distance (MRD) from the center of the upper lid margin to the corneal reflex — which is more accurate than measuring the palpebral fissure width.
  • It is graded as follows:
Mild
1–2 mm
Drooping of 1–2 mm
Moderate
3 mm
Dropping of 3 mm
Severe
> 4 mm
Dropping of more than 4 mm
Technique Measure with the patient in the primary position of gaze and the frontalis relaxed — an elevated brow masks the true degree of ptosis.
