Part II · Secretory system

The lacrimal secretory system

It consists of

  • 1) The main orbital or superior lacrimal gland.
  • 2) The palpebral or inferior lacrimal gland.
  • 3) The accessory lacrimal glands.
Divide the main gland sits in the lacrimal fossa above the globe and is separated from the palpebral part by the levator aponeurosis.

The accessory lacrimal glands

Microscopic in size, opening into the conjunctiva by fine ductules:

  • Krause’s gland: located in the upper and lower fornices.
  • Wolfring gland: located in the palpebral conjunctiva opposite the mid-tarsus.
  • Meibomian gland.
  • Zeis gland.
  • Goblet cells of the conjunctiva.
