Part I · Blepharitis

Complications of anterior blepharitis

By structure

  • Conjunctiva: chronic conjunctivitis.
  • Cornea: marginal corneal ulcers, superficial punctate keratitis.
  • Lacrimal: punctal occlusion.
  • Eyelid:
    • Trichiasis — due to scarring at the lashes’ roots (healed ulcer).
    • Madarosis — due to destruction of hair follicles.
    • Tylosis — with destruction of the sharp posterior border of the lid margin, leading to epiphora and then initiating the vicious circle of epiphora – eczema – ectropion.
    • Multiple styes.

The vicious circle

epiphora → eczema → ectropion → more epiphora

N.B. A scarred, rounded posterior lid margin loses its capillary action, so tears overflow onto the skin and the dermatitis itself worsens the malposition.
