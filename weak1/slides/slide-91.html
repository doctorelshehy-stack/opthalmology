Part III · Thyroid eye disease

Treatment of TED

  1. Treatment of thyrotoxicosis.
  2. Artificial tear substitutes for corneal protection.
  3. Lateral tarsorrhaphy in severe lagophthalmos.
  4. Systemic steroids.
  5. Orbital decompression in advanced cases, e.g. optic neuropathy.
N.B. In TED, lid retraction and proptosis may lead to exposure keratopathy — lubrication is the first line of defence, decompression is reserved for sight-threatening optic neuropathy.
