Part II · Dacryocystitis

Acute dacryocystitis — complications and treatment

Complications

  • Lacrimal fistula: the sac opens anteriorly through the skin.
  • Pyocele and chronic dacryocystitis.
  • Orbital cellulitis and cavernous sinus thrombosis.

Treatment

  • Acute phase: antibiotics (systemic and local), hot fomentation, analgesics and nasal decongestant drops.
  • Pyocele: incision and drainage of the abscess.
  • Lacrimal fistula: fistulectomy with dacryocystorhinostomy.
N.B. Never incise an acute sac in the acute inflamed stage unless a pointing abscess is present — wait for quiescence before a definitive DCR.
