Part I · Chalazion

Chalazion — definition and etiology

Definition

A localized chronic inflammatory lipo-granuloma of a Meibomian gland.

Etiology

Unknown, but may be:

  • A granuloma due to irritation of the Meibomian gland by its retained secretions, following obstruction of the duct secondary to fibrosis or desquamation of the epithelium of its duct (vitamin A deficiency).
  • Low grade bacterial infection.
  • Residual hordeolum internum.

Clinical picture — symptoms

  • Painless swelling under the skin of the eyelid.
  • Pain occurs only if it becomes infected (acute chalazion).

Signs

  • Localized, firm, painless, not tender, with the skin freely mobile over it.
  • The palpebral conjunctiva is red or bluish grey over it.
