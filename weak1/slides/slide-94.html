Part III · Proptosis

Proptosis — definition and etiology

Definition: abnormal protrusion of the eyeball outside the orbital rim.

A) Unilateral proptosis

  • 1) Thyroid ophthalmopathy (the most common).
  • 2) (Congenital) developmental: dermoid cyst.
  • 3) Traumatic: as in retrobulbar hemorrhage, surgical emphysema.
  • 4) Inflammatory: orbital cellulitis (acute) & orbital pseudotumor (chronic).
  • 5) Vascular: orbital varices and carotid-cavernous fistula.
  • 6) (Miscellaneous) cystic lesions: hydatid cyst.

7) (Tumor) neoplastic

PrimarySecondary
Benign: hemangiomaIntraocular malignancy: melanoma
Malignant: rhabdomyosarcoma & optic nerve gliomaFrom adjacent structure: paranasal sinuses; metastases

B) Bilateral proptosis

  • 1) Thyroid ophthalmopathy (the most common cause).
  • 2) Cavernous sinus thrombosis.
