Part I · Minute anatomy

Layers in detail 1 – 4

1) The skin

Thin, elastic and loosely adherent to the underlying tissues.

2) Subcutaneous areolar tissue

Containing no fat.

3) The muscles layer

  • Striated (voluntary) muscles: orbicularis oculi and levator palpebrae superioris.
  • Non-striated (involuntary) muscles: Müller’s muscle in the upper eyelid and the lower lid retractors in the lower eyelid.

4) Submuscular areolar tissue

  • Lies between the orbicularis muscle and the tarsal plate.
  • Contains the peripheral arterial arcade and the main sensory nerves to the eyelid.
  • Therefore it is necessary to inject local anesthetic to the eyelid deep to the orbicularis.
Clinical A subcutaneous injection stays superficial to the muscle and misses the neurovascular plane; the block must be deposited deep to the orbicularis.
