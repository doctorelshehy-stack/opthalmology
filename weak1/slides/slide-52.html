Part I · Ptosis assessment

2) Determine the levator function power

Technique

  • Correct the head position, then ask the patient to look down and fix the eyebrow against the superior orbital margin by the thumb to prevent elevation by the frontalis muscle; then ask the patient to look up and measure the elevation of the lid margin (in mm) with a ruler.

Interpretation — levator function is graded as

Poor
4 mm or less
Fair
5–11 mm
Good
11–14 mm
Normal
15 mm or more
Why it matters The amount of ptosis tells you how much to correct; the levator function tells you which operation is possible.
