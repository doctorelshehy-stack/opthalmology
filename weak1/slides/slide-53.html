Part I · Ptosis treatment

Treatment of ptosis

A) Congenital — age of interference

  • Mild to moderate: wait till the school age (5–6 years).
  • Severe: as early as possible to avoid amblyopia.

B) Acquired — treatment of the cause

  • Mechanical: remove the cause (tumor or multiple chalazia).
  • Hysterical: psychoanalysis.
  • Myasthenia gravis: prostigmine.
  • Paralytic or traumatic causes: wait for 6 months, then re-evaluate the ptosis.
  • Senile: levator aponeurosis repair or levator resection.
