Part III · Globe position

Enophthalmos and atrophia bulbi

Enophthalmos

Definition: retraction of the eyeball in the orbit.

Causes

  • Microphthalmos.
  • Orbital floor fracture.
  • Following orbital inflammations.
  • Cicatrizing orbital tumors.
  • Removal of orbital contents surgically.

Atrophia bulbi

Etiology

  • Severe ocular trauma.
  • Retinal detachment (long-standing).
  • Severe ocular inflammation (endophthalmitis).

Clinically

  • Shrunken globe.
  • Loss of vision.

Management

Enucleation if blind and painful.
