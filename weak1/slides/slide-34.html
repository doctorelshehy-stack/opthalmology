Part I · Entropion

1) Congenital entropion

Features

  • Common in the lower eyelid.

Causes

  • Hypertrophy of the orbicularis muscle.
  • Dehiscence or absence of the lower lid retractors.

Treatment

Reattachment of the lower lid retractors to the lower tarsal border.

Why Without the posterior tether the small lower tarsus rotates inwards; re-establishing the retractor insertion restores the vertical stability of the lid.
