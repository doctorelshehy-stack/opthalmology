Part III · Orbital infection

Orbital cellulitis — complications and treatment

Complications

OcularIntracranial
Exposure keratopathyMeningitis
Orbital abscess & panophthalmitisBrain abscess
Central retinal vein or artery occlusionCavernous sinus thrombosis

In severe untreated cases systemic complications may develop, e.g. septicemia and pyemia.

Differential diagnosis

  • Cavernous sinus thrombosis.
  • Acute suppurative dacryoadenitis.
  • Preseptal cellulitis.

Treatment

  • Hospitalization is necessary for close follow-up.
  • Topical and systemic parenteral broad-spectrum antibiotics.
  • Hot foments.
N.B. If an abscess is formed, it should be drained.
