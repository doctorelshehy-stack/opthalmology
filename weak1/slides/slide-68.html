Part II · Epiphora treatment

Treatment of epiphora

1) Treatment of the cause

e.g. ectropion and nasal causes of epiphora.

2) Stenosis of puncta and canaliculi

  • Dilatation and probing.
  • One snip ampullotomy: a vertical snip is made in the posterior wall of the canal.
  • Laser punctoplasty.

3) Obstruction of puncta and canaliculi

  • Two snip operation: a triangle is removed from the posterior wall of the canaliculus.
  • Conjunctivo-dacryocystorhinostomy: the conjunctiva is connected to the nose by a glass tube to overcome the obstruction.

4) Obstruction of the nasolacrimal duct

  • Congenital: hydrostatic massage; dilation and probing; dacryocystorhinostomy.
  • Acquired: dilation and probing usually fails; dacryocystorhinostomy; dacryocystectomy.
