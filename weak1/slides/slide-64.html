Part II · Epiphora

Etiology of epiphora

1) Failure of the pump mechanism

  • Irregular posterior sharp margin of the eyelid.
  • Ectropion.
  • Paralysis of the orbicularis oculi (Horner’s muscle) in Bell’s palsy.

2) Obstruction — at the puncta and canaliculi

  • Congenital absence, post-trachomatous, fungal or herpes scarring, or post-traumatic stenosis.

Obstruction — lacrimal sac

  • Congenital absence, dacryocystitis, fracture or lacrimal tumors.

Obstruction — nasolacrimal duct

  • Imperforated Hasner’s valve.
  • Vein congestion — 50% of cases of epiphora.
  • Fungal infection (streptothrix).
  • Fracture or tumors.

Obstruction — nose

  • Adenosis and nasal polypi, deviated septum, hypertrophy of the inferior turbinate, rhinoscleroma.
