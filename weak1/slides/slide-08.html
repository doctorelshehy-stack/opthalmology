Part I · Minute anatomy

Layer 5 — The fibrous layer

1) The tarsal plates

  • Dense fibrous tissue forming the skeleton of the lids.
  • Upper tarsus height 8–12 mm; lower tarsus about 4–5 mm.
  • Laterally and medially the plates fuse with the medial and lateral palpebral ligaments (canthal tendons).

2) The meibomian (tarsal) glands

  • Elongated sebaceous glands embedded within the tarsal plate.
  • 30 in the upper lid and 25 in the lower lid.
  • Orifices open on the lid margin behind the grey line.

Function of the meibomian secretion

They form an oily film over the moistened cornea to retard evaporation of tears and prevent overflow of tears over the skin.

3) The orbital septum

  • Separates the eyelids from the orbit.
  • Arises from the periosteum of the orbital rim and attaches to the levator aponeurosis in the upper eyelid and to the lower tarsal border in the lower lid.
  • Acts as a diaphragm keeping the fat within the orbit.
