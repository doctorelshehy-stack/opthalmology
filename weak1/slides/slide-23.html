Part I · Gland inflammation

Inflammations of the eyelid glands (Hordeolum)

Definition: a localized acute infection arising within the glands at the eyelid margin.

1) Hordeolum externum (Stye)

Acute suppurative inflammation of the Zeiss gland — at the root of an eyelash.

2) Hordeolum internum (Infected chalazion)

Acute suppurative inflammation of the Meibomian gland — within the tarsus, away from the lashes.
