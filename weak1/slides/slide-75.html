Part II · Dry eye

Dry eye — definition and causes

Definition: it is partial or absolute deficiency in the aqueous tear production.

  • 1) Congenital absence of the lacrimal gland (alacrimia).
  • 2) Traumatic; surgical removal.
  • 3) Inflammation of the lacrimal gland, e.g. sarcoidosis.
  • 4) Tumors of the lacrimal gland, e.g. mixed lacrimal gland tumor.
  • 5) Keratoconjunctivitis sicca (most common) — an autoimmune disease (due to abnormal immuno-globulins) that leads to atrophy and fibrosis of the lacrimal gland, usually in females; it may take the form of Sjogren’s syndrome if associated with dry mouth (xerostomia) and polyarthritis.
  • 6) Mucin deficiency (secreted by goblet cells) due to conjunctival scarring (inflammatory), e.g. trachoma, chemical burn, and vitamin A deficiency (xerosis).
  • 7) Paralytic: leading to improper lid closure, e.g. lagophthalmos, thyrotoxicosis and facial palsy.
  • 8) Degenerative: senile atrophy of the gland.
  • 9) Iatrogenic: belladonna and anti-anxiety drugs.
