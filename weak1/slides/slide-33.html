Part I · Lid malposition

Entropion — definition and types

Definition

Usually defined as rolling in of the lid margin with the eyelashes rubbing against the eyeball.

Compares with ectropion = rolling out of the lid margin.

Etiological types

  1. Congenital.
  2. Cicatricial entropion.
  3. Spastic.
  4. Senile (involutional).

Clinical picture: as in acquired mal-directed lashes — foreign body sensation, pain, lacrimation, photophobia, blepharospasm and corneal irritation.
