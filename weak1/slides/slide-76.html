Part II · Dry eye

Dry eye — clinical picture and diagnosis

Symptoms

  • Irritation, foreign body sensation and burning sensation in severe cases, especially on waking up.

Signs

  • Deficient pre-corneal tear film with scanty marginal tear strip at the lower lid margin.
  • Punctate epithelial erosions, filamentary keratitis with filaments of mucous and desquamated epithelial debris attached to the cornea.
  • Conjunctival wrinkling and Bitôt spots (keratinized lusterless conjunctiva in the temporal or nasal site).

Diagnosis

  • Schirmer’s test — measures aqueous tear secretion.
  • Tear film break-up time (BUT) — measures tear film stability.
  • Rose Bengal staining of devitalized epithelial cells.
Applied Schirmer measures quantity, BUT measures quality — a patient may have normal volume but an unstable film.
