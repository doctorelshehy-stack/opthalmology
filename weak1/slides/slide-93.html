Part III · Vascular

Cavernous sinus thrombosis — clinical and treatment

Etiology

  • Spread of infection along tributaries and connections, as in orbital cellulitis.
  • Blood born infection.

Clinical picture

  • General: fever, malaise, headache, associated with cerebral symptoms.
  • Local: severe orbital pain; diplopia due to paralysis of the oculomotors.

Complications

  • Meningitis, brain abscess.

Signs

  • Lid edema.
  • Conjunctival chemosis.
  • Rapidly increasing axial proptosis.
  • Ophthalmoplegia.
  • Edema in the mastoid region.
  • Involvement of the contralateral eye.

Treatment

  1. Hospitalization.
  2. IV intensive broad-spectrum antibiotics.
  3. IV heparin 5000 i.u. every 6 hours.
  4. Treatment of the causative septic focus.
