Part II · Watering of the eye

Lacrimation vs Epiphora

Watering of the eye: overflow of tears on the cheek. It includes lacrimation and epiphora.

Lacrimation

Due to excessive or over-secretion of tears while the lacrimal passages are normal.

  • Psychic and emotional conditions, as weeping.
  • Synkinesis with the 9th and 10th cranial nerves, as yawning and vomiting.
  • Reflexes:
    • Bright light (afferent optic nerve).
    • Irritation of trigeminal nerve endings, e.g. FB in the conjunctiva or cornea.
    • Irritation of the nasal mucosa by dust, smokes or inflammation.

Epiphora

Due to obstruction of the lacrimal passage with normal tear secretion.

  • Failure of the pump mechanism.
  • Obstruction of the drainage system at any level: puncta and canaliculi, lacrimal sac, nasolacrimal duct, or nose.
