Part I · Entropion

3) Spastic entropion

Pathogenesis

  • Occurs in young age due to spasm of Riolan’s muscle as a reflex (blepharospasm) of acute ocular inflammation or chronic corneal irritation.
  • More in the lower lid — a small and thin tarsus that rolls easily.

Treatment — remove the cause

  • Removal or treatment of the predisposing cause.

Temporary measures

  • T-shaped adhesive plaster.
  • Botulinum toxin injection to the orbicularis to weaken it (chemical denervation).
  • Everting sutures: 3–4 mattress sutures, starting at the lower fornix to be tied on the skin below the eyelid margin.

Permanent measures

Only for chronic resistant cases:

  • Skin and muscle operation: excision of an elliptical area of skin with the underlying muscle of Riolan close to the lid margin.
