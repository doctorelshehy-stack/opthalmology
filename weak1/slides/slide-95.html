Part III · Proptosis work-up

Clinical approach — history

a) Past history

  • Orbital trauma.
  • Paranasal sinuses diseases.

b) Present history for proptosis

  • Onset: acute in retrobulbar hemorrhage and slow in benign tumors.
  • Course:
    • Progressive in malignant tumors.
    • Regressive in inflammations.
    • Intermittent in orbital varices.

Other symptoms

  • Other ocular symptoms: pain and diplopia.
  • General symptoms as in thyrotoxicosis.
Rule of onset sudden onset → vascular (hemorrhage, fistula, varix); slowly progressive → tumour; regressing → inflammation.
