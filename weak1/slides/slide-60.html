Part II · Tear film

Functions of tears

Nutrition
Supply the cornea by glucose and O2 uptake, and a media for gaseous exchange between the cornea and air.
Optical
The pre-corneal tear film maintains optical uniformity of the corneal surface by filling up any small irregularities in the corneal surface epithelium.
Wetting
Wetting effect protects against dryness.
Protection
Mechanical washout of small foreign bodies, and antimicrobial action.
Remember nutrition · optical · protection — a deficient film produces both dry-eye symptoms and optical blur.
