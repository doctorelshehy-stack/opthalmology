Anatomy of the orbit

Definition: the orbit is a four-sided pyramidal space on both sides of the midline that contains the globe and the surrounding tissues.

Seven bones comprise the orbit

  • Ethmoid.
  • Frontal.
  • Lacrimal.
  • Maxillary.
  • Palatine.
  • Sphenoid.
  • Zygomatic.

Geometry

  • The adult lateral orbital walls are approximately 90° from each other, or 45° from the sagittal midline plane.
  • In contrast, the medial walls are parallel.
