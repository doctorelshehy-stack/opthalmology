Part III · Anatomy

Walls and rim of the orbit

Walls

  • Roof: mainly formed by the orbital plate of the frontal bone.
  • Floor: composed of the maxillary bone, the palatine bone and the zygoma.
  • Medial orbital wall: the thinnest (0.2 to 0.4 mm) and the smallest; the ethmoid bone makes up most of it, with contributions from the maxillary and lacrimal bones.
  • Lateral orbital wall: bounded by the superior and inferior orbital fissures.

Orbital rim

  • The anterior opening to the orbit, with a rectangular configuration.
Applied The thin medial wall explains why ethmoid sinusitis readily spreads into the orbit, and why blow-out fractures involve the orbital floor and medial wall.
