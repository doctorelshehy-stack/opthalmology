Part I · Lagophthalmos

Complications and treatment

Complications

  • Cornea: exposure keratitis develops, which may end in corneal ulceration, perforation and loss of the eye.
  • Conjunctiva: may be exposed, dry and xerotic.

Treatment aim

It should aim at protecting the cornea.

1) Temporary protection

  • In mild degrees ointment is instilled at night.
  • Closure of the lids by narrow strips of plaster.
  • A contact lens can be used when the lids cannot be brought together.

2) Surgical treatment

  • Lateral tarsorrhaphy: reduces the width of the palpebral fissure by uniting the edges of the lids at the lateral canthus.
  • Median tarsorrhaphy: union of the whole lid margin, sometimes indicated in cases with severe lagophthalmos.
