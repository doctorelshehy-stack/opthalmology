Conjunctiva · Allergic conjunctivitis

Allergic conjunctivitis and vernal kerato-conjunctivitis

3) Allergic Conjunctivitis

It is a hypersensitivity to:

  • An exogenous allergen: as in
    • Vernal kerato-conjunctivitis (VKC).
    • Giant papillary conjunctivitis.
    • Hay fever conjunctivitis.
    • Atopic kerato-conjunctivitis.
  • An endogenous allergen: as in phlyctenular keratoconjunctivitis.

Vernal Kerato-Conjunctivitis (Spring Catarrh)

It is a chronic bilateral recurrent seasonal allergic conjunctivitis. Usually affecting children (boys more) and showing exacerbation during warm season (summer and not spring). Spring is a misnomer.

Etiology

  • It is allergy to unknown exogenous factors (IgE mediated) as indicated by the accompanying eosinophilia. e.g. ultraviolet rays, heat, dust, pollen grains.
