Treatment of Senile Cataract

1) Time of the operation

This depends on several factors:

  • V.A of the patient.
  • His occupation & visual needs, his activities.
  • And finally, the stage of cataract.

2) Which eye to start with in bilateral cases?

  • The more mature or the more liable to develop complications (intumescent form or the hypermature).
  • If equally affected,
    • It isn’t recommended to operate on the 2 eyes on the same setting.
    • A 1-week interval between the 2 operations is Ok (fear of operative/hospital infection).
