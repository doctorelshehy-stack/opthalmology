Cataract · Congenital & Developmental

1) Congenital & Developmental Cataract

Definition

It is the opacity of the lens or its capsule dating since birth or soon after it.

Causes

  • Hereditary, i.e. genetically determined.
  • Deficient O2 with fetal hypoxia as in cases with placental hemorrhage.
  • Maternal malnutrition during pregnancy or infantile malnutrition (vit. D, Ca, proteins).
  • Maternal infection particularly during the first trimester.
  • Metabolic: Galactosemia, Galactokinase deficiency, mannosidosis, hypoglycemia.
  • Systemic syndromes e.g. Lowe syndrome.

General features of congenital cataract

  • It is usually bilateral.
  • It is partial.
  • It is stationary, but rarely it progresses to be total as in rubella cataract.
  • It is soft cataract.
