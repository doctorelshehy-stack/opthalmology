Refraction · Hypermetropia

Hypermetropia: treatment

1) Optical Treatment

a) Glasses: (Spherical convex or plus lenses).

Children with high errors causing symptoms:

Full correction i.e. all the manifest hypermetropia (all the total hypermetropia in case of accommodative esotropia).

Adults:

Highest tolerated plus lens will be prescribed - Full correction after 6 months.

Old age:

Full correction (accommodation is weak).

b) Contact lenses.

2) Refractive surgery

  1. Corneal refractive surgery: Excimer laser (LASIK - PRK)
  2. Lens refractive surgery: Phakic IOL – Refractive lens exchange.
