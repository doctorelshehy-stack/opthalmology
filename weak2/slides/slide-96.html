2) Complicated Cataract to a Systemic Eye Disease (Presenile Cataract)

Definition

It is acquired lens opacity due to a systemic disease (usually bilateral).

Causes

  • Diabetes mellitus.
  • Hypoparathyroidism.
  • Galactosemia.
  • Myotonic dystrophy.
  • Atopic dermatitis.
  • Neurofibromatosis.
