Causes of corneal transparency

1) Anatomical factors:

  • Non-keratinized epithelium, which is also uniform regular, and its two surfaces are parallel to the surface of the cornea.
  • Regularly arranged tightly packed lamellae of the stroma with a uniform refractive index.
  • Avascularity (no blood vessels and no lymphatic).
  • The nerve fibers are non-myelinated.

2) Physiological factors:

  • Detergence, which means that the corneal lamellae are not fully hydrated.
  • Mucopolysaccharides content of the stroma.
  • Uniform refractive index.
