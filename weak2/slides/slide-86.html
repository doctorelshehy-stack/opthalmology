Cataract · Senile Cortical

2) Immature Stage of Senile Cortical Cataract

Clinical picture

Symptoms:

  • Painless diminution of vision mainly by the night, i.e. night blindness.

Signs:

  • V.A: drop of vision but usually better than hand movement.
  • Slit lamp examination through a fully dilated pupil will show incomplete grayish white uniform opacification of the deep cortex.
  • Iris shadow is present in the presences of a transparent medium between the iris and the lens opacity.

Intumescent Form of Immature Cortical Cataract

  • It is due to rapid degeneration leading to hydration of the lens (water influx from the aqueous).
  • The swollen lens may lead to secondary pupillary block glaucoma (Phacomorphic glaucoma).
