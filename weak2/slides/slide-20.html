Treatment of the resistant corneal ulcers

In addition to the above lines for treatment, the following may be needed:

  • Subconjunctival injection of antibiotics: to bypass the epithelial barrier and reach the ulcer in high concentration.
  • Collagen shields: Used as effective high dose drug delivery system in the form of contact lens that dissolves spontaneously over 24, 48- or 72hours. It results in 30-40-fold increase in antibiotic penetration into the aqueous.
  • Paracentesis: Puncture & evacuation of the AC using a special knife.
  • Amniotic membrane graft: it dissolves over a period of time and help healing by providing substrate material to cover the ulcer.
  • Conjunctival flap: A bulbar conjunctival flap near the ulcer is used to cover the ulcer. The highly vascular conjunctiva will provide defense, nutrition for the ulcer.
  • Tarsorrhaphy: Lateral and even permanent tarsorrhaphy may sometimes be needed in special cases of facial palsy, exophthalmos and neuroparalysis.
  • Therapeutic keratoplasty: either lamellar or penetrating.
