Cornea · Fungal ulcer

Diagnosis and treatment

Diagnosis: Corneal scraping followed by:

  • The use of Gram and Giemsa stain.
  • Culture on Sabraud´s broth and agar.
  • Blood agar & brain heart infusion both may be essential.
N.B:Corneal biopsy by superficial keratotomy may be needed in deep lesions.

Treatment:

MedicalSurgical (in Resistant Cases)
1) Epithelial debridement.1) Superficial keratectomy.
2) Antifungal drugs.2) Conjunctival flap.
3) Cycloplegic eye drops as atropine.3) Keratoplasty.
