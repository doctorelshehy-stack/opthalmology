Purulent conjunctivitis (P.C, Acute Blenorrhoea)

Definition: An acute suppurative inflammation of the conjunctiva, which is characterized by:

  • Hyperemia and copious purulent discharge.
  • Tendency to corneal involvement, enlarged lymph nodes.
  • And constitutional symptoms (e.g. Fever).

Types

  • Epidemic type: the most common and occur in summer (July - August).
  • Genital type:
    • From autoinfection by hand from genital gonorrhea (male suffer more).
    • And from maternal passages during labor (ophthalmia neonatorum) (see later).

Etiology

  • The causative organisms:
    • Gonococcus (80%): they are gram negative diplococci.
    • Other organisms (20%): e.g. streptococci, staphylococci.
  • Mode of transmission: Contagious:
    • From eye to eye.
    • From genital organs (gonorrhea).
