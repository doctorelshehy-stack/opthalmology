Refraction · Myopia

Myopia: clinical picture — symptoms

Symptoms

  1. Indistinct far vision.
  2. Other symptoms: Only occur with high myopia.

Other symptoms

  1. Eye strain (asthenopia) and may be strabismus after prolonged near work.
  2. Photopsia & musca volitantes (Fluidy vitreous containing some opacity).
  3. Defective night vision.
  4. Visual field defects.
