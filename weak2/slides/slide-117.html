Refraction · Astigmatism

Treatment of astigmatism

1) Optical

a) Glasses:

Simple astigmatismCompound or mixed astigmatism
  • Can be corrected by cylindrical lens with its axis perpendicular to the meridian to be corrected.
  • First changed to simple astigmatism by suitable spherical lens.
  • Then astigmatic error is corrected by cylindrical lens (sphero-cylindrical lens).

b) Contact lenses:

Regular astigmatismIrregular astigmatism
  • Soft or Toric lenses.
  • Rigid (gas permeable) lenses.

2) Surgical

  • A. Regular astigmatism: Excimer laser (LASIK - PRK).
  • B. Keratoconus: Corneal cross-linking – ICR – ICRS – Keratoplasty.
  • C. Corneal opacities: Phototherapeutic keratectomy (PTK) – Keratoplasty.
