Cataract · Surgery

Surgical Techniques, IOL Implantation and Complications

A. Surgical Techniques for Cataract Extraction

  • 1) Phacoemulsification:
    • Based on emulsification of the lens by the ultrasonic vibration.
    • Then, aspiration of the emulsion of the fragmented lens matter.
  • 2) Extracapsular cataract extraction (ECCE):
    • Based on surgical delivery of the lens nucleus out of the eye through the anterior capsulotomy followed by irrigation/aspiration of the remaining lens matter, leaving an intact posterior lens capsule.
  • 3) Intracapsular cataract extraction (ICCE):
    • Based on removal of the lens out of the eye within its capsule.
    • It may be used in extensive Lens subluxation.
    • It is very limited nowadays due to high incidence of complications as vitreous loss, retinal detachment and cystoid macular edema.

B. Intraocular Lens Implantation

  • Primary IOL implantation: In the same operation of cataract extraction.
  • Secondary IOL implantation: If operative conditions weren’t suitable in the first surgery, a second operation is required for IOL implantation.

Complication of surgery

  • Vitreous Loss.
  • Vitreous Hemorrhage.
  • Increased Eye Pressure.
  • Uveitis.
  • Endophthalmitis.
  • Retinal Detachment.
