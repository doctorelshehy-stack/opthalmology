Nutrition and functions of the cornea

Nutrition of the cornea: by diffusion form:

  • Limbal capillaries.
  • The aqueous, the main supply of glucose.
  • The tears supplying O2 and NaCl. (This is the main corneal supply of O2).

Functions of the cornea:

1) Optical:

  • It is transparent, allow the passage of light.
  • It is the main refractive medium of the eye (42 diopters).

2) Protective:

  • Being richly supplied by sensory nerves which are easily excited it can initiate the very quick protective lid closure.
