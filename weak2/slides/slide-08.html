The Limbus

The corneo-scleral junction, the histological changes which occur at the limbus are:

  • Corneal epithelium becomes continuous with the conjunctival epithelium.
  • Bowman's membrane ends at the limbus.
  • Stroma becomes continuous with the sclera.
  • Descemet’s membrane becomes continuous with the trabecular meshwork of the angle.
  • Endothelium is continuous with that at the angle of the AC and on the iris.
