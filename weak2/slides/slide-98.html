After Cataract (Posterior Capsule Opacification, PCO)

Definition

It is posterior capsular lens opacity which forms or persists after either cataract surgery or partially absorbed traumatic cataract.

Clinically

  • History of trauma or cataract surgery.
  • Symptoms: painless progressive diminution of vision.

Signs

After cataract is seen and is in the form of Opaque lens fibers:

  • Elschnig’s pearls: transparent soap bubble lens cells.
  • Capsular fibrosis: 2-6 months after surgery. It may involve the posterior capsule or remnants of the anterior capsule.

Treatment

YAG laser or Surgical capsulotomy depending to its density.
