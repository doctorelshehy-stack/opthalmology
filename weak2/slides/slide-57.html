Conjunctiva · Trachoma — grading

WHO Classification of trachoma

It is recent based on evaluating the severity of trachoma and widely used nowadays.

TF: Trachomatous Follicles

  • F1 Trivial (insignificant/absent)
  • F2 mild
  • F3 Moderate
  • F4 Severe

TI: Trachomatous Intense

  • Diffuse inflammation of conjunctiva obscuring 50% of large deep tarsal Vessels.

TS: Trachomatous Scarring of conjunctiva.

  • Grade 1, 2, 3

TT: Trachomatous Trichiasis

  • At least one in-turning lash or history of removal.

CO: Corneal Opacities

  • At least obscuring part of the pupil margin or causing vision 6/18 or less
