Conjunctiva · Bacterial conjunctivitis

ON: complications and treatment

Complications: As P.C.

Treatment

Prophylaxis:

  • Treatment of mother before labor.
  • Washing babies from above downward.
  • Crede’s method: by antibiotics eye drops.

Curative:

  • As P.C. but more serious due to thin cornea, no tears and no lysozyme.
N.B. the lacrimal gland starts to secrete tears, at least, 2 weeks after birth so any discharge during the 1st week in a neonate must be suspected.
