Conjunctiva · Normal anatomy

Histology and vascular system

Histologically: two layers

  • The epithelium:
    • Non-keratinized stratified squamous epithelium.
    • It contains goblet cells that secrete mucous.
  • The Substantia propria: Is composed of two layers:
    • a) Superficial adenoid layer which consists of loose C.T. containing leukocytes.
    • b) Deep fibrous layer which is much denser and contains blood vessels and nerves.

Vascular system of the conjunctiva

  • Arterial supply:
    • The anterior conjunctival arteries which are branches of the anterior ciliary arteries
    • The posterior conjunctival arteries which are branches from the palpebral arteries and anastomose freely with the anterior conjunctival arteries.
  • Venous drainage:
    • Into the palpebral and ophthalmic veins.
  • Lymph drainage:
    • The lateral part of the conjunctiva drains into the parotid and preauricular nodes.
    • The medial part drains into the submaxillary group.
    • Both drain into the upper deep cervical group.
