Refraction · ILOS

Intended learning objectives

  1. To understand the concept of emmetropia & ametropia.
  2. To know the different types of refractive errors.
  3. To know the etiology & different types of myopia, hypermetropia & astigmatism.
  4. To understand the effect of accommodation on the different refractive errors and how it affects the components of hypermetropia.
  5. to know the clinical picture & possible complications of refractive errors.
  6. To understand how to estimate the state of refraction and practice the basic steps of retinoscopy.
  7. To know the main lines of treatment of refractive errors and understand the basics of refractive surgery.
  8. To understand the causes of aphakia and the optical problems caused by monocular aphakia and the methods of correction.
  9. To understand the pathogenesis of presbyopia & how to prescribe reading spectacles.
