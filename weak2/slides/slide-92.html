Outline for Preoperative Examination of Cataract

Outline

  • 1) General examination.
  • 2) Laboratory investigations.
  • 3) Local examination:

Local examination

Complete ocular examination including the following:

  • VA: Unaided & Aided. “No PL = No Surgery”.
  • IOP: For Associated glaucoma (guarded prognosis)
  • Conjunctiva: For possible infection.
  • Cornea: Clarity is vital for safe surgery.
  • Anterior Chamber: For depth and inflammatory activity.
  • Iris & Pupil: Pupillary reaction to light is vital to assess optic neuropathy.
  • Lens: Cataract type, grade and lens stability.
  • Fundus: To ensure normal optic disc, macula & vasculature as well as retinal periphery (Predict the prognosis).
