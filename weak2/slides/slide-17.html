Corneal fistula

  • Occurs in central perforated corneal ulcers due to delayed healing.
  • This result from repeated dislodgement of clot closing the perforation due to chronic straining or increased IOP.
  • Delayed healing of the perforation result in creeping of the epithelium over the edges of the ulcer which will never heal by its own (corneal fistula).
Clinically:Small central perforated ulcer with river green sign, soft ocular tension and lost anterior chamber.
Complications:Infection with endophthalmitis, muscular edema and papilledema due to hypotony, anterior polar cataract and peripheral anterior synechia.
