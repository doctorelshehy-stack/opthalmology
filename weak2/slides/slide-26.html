Herpes zoster ophthalmicus (HZO)

Definition, cause and clinical picture

Definition: It is a primary infective lesion of the cornea due to affection of the ophthalmic division of the Trigeminal nerve by the herpes zoster virus.

Cause:

  • The (HZV) is a neurotropic virus which travels down along the ophthalmic division affecting any of its terminal branches, infects the Gasserian ganglion of the Trigeminal nerve.

Clinical picture:

Incubation Period: 12 – 14 days.

Symptoms:

  • Fever, malaise, neuralgic pain (severe).
