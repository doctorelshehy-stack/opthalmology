Cornea · Hypopyon ulcer

Clinical picture — symptoms

Symptoms:

“DD for Painful drop of Vision”

  • Pain: stitching. It is caused by exposure of the nerve endings to the atmosphere.
  • Defective vision: Especially in central ulcers.
  • Photophobia: Discomfort on exposure to light due to the accompanying iritis and miosis.
  • Lacrimation: Excessive lacrimation due to reflex stimulation of the lacrimal gland.
  • Blepharospasm: Severe reflex spasm of the orbicularis oculi muscle with eyelid movement due to mechanical irritation of the bare nerve endings.
  • Halos around light: Caused by corneal edema.
