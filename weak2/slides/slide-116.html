Refraction · Astigmatism

Signs of astigmatism

Signs

  1. Visual acuity: The opening of the rings in certain directions are noticed but not in the others.
  2. Corneal exam: Nebulae, keratoconus.... etc.
  3. Placido's disc: Distorted circles on the cornea.
  4. Ophthalmoscopy: The optic disc is oval due to the inequality of the dioptric powers in the two meridians.
  5. Retinoscopy:
    • The most reliable method to diagnose and measure astigmatism in which different diopteric powers are used for neutralizing the principal meridians.
    • Scissor reflex (irregular astigmatism).
    • Spinning reflex (keratoconus).
  6. Keratometry: It measures the power and axis of the highest and least meridians of the cornea.
  7. Corneal topography: The most sensitive but not used routinely (expensive).
