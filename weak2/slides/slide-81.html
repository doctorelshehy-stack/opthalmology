Cataract · Congenital Types

Types of Congenital & Developmental Cataract

1) Anterior Polar Cataract. 2) Zonular or lamellar cataract. 3) Posterior Polar Cataract. 4) Blue Dotted Cataract. 5) Coronary Cataract. 6) Rubella Total Cataract.

1) Anterior Polar Cataract:

Symptoms:

  • Usually asymptomatic because it is usually small and away from nodal point.

2) Zonular or lamellar cataract:

Clinical picture:

Symptoms:

  • It is symptomatic, so the mother will notice:
    • 1) White color of the pupil “Leukocoria”.
    • 2) The child does not see well “Poor fixation”.

Signs:

  • 1) V.A (if possible): markedly dropped.
  • 2) Oblique illumination through a fully dilated pupil will show:
    • lens opacity: grayish white, disc shaped at the central part while the periphery of the lens is clear.
    • Radial riders or spokes project from the main opacity (a steering wheel shape).
  • 3) Slit lamp through a fully dilated pupil.
  • 4) Red reflex through a fully dilated pupil will show:
    • Peripheral bright red reflex due to the clear lens periphery.
    • Central dim red reflex due to the central lens opacity.
