Conjunctiva · Bacterial conjunctivitis

ON: clinical pictures

AgentTime of onsetCharacteristics
Chemicals e.g. silver nitrate drops, savlon. Within hours
  • Self-limited, mild, with serous discharge.
  • Lasts 24-36 hours.
N. Gonorrhea 24-48 hours
  • Hyperacute, copious, purulent discharge.
  • Lid swelling and chemosis are common.
  • Treated by systemic Benzyl penicillin 50.000 u/kg in two divided doses for 7 days or single IM injection of Cefotaxime 100 mg/kg and topical tobramycin- fluoquinolone drops
Chlamydia trachomatis. (Inclusion blenorrhoea) 5-12 day
  • Mild to moderate, purulent discharge.
  • Redness (more in the palpebral conj.)
  • Treated by local terramycin ointment and systemic erythromycin 50 mg/kg/day for 3 weeks
Non- gonococcal bacteria 5-30 days
  • Variable according to the organisms.
  • Only mild injection of the conjunctiva.
Viral (herpes simplex) Within 2 weeks
  • Serosanguinous discharge.
  • Sometimes, vesicular rash on the lids.
  • Treated by anti-herpetic drops or ointments.
