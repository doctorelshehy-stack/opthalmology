Refraction · Myopia

Myopia: complications & optical treatment

Complications

  1. Squint: Apparent convergent and true divergent.
  2. Posterior vitreous detachment (PVD).
  3. Retinal detachment: Due to tear or hole formation in areas of chorio-retinal degeneration.
  4. Macular hemorrhage, or hole.
  5. Complicated cataract.
  6. Pigmentary glaucoma: Due to pigment deposition in trabecular meshwork.
  7. Consecutive optic atrophy: Due to advanced chorio-retinal degeneration.

1) Optical Treatment

  1. Glasses: (concave or minus lenses): The least tolerable minus lens.
  2. Contact lenses.
