Cataract · 2) Acquired Cataract

1) Senile Cataract

Definition

It is an acquired progressive opacity that occurs in old aged group; above 50 years in the absence of any local or systemic disease.

Features

  • It is a progressive hard cataract.
  • It is bilateral but may start in one eye first "asymmetrical".

Incidence

  • Age: above 50 years.
  • Sex: both are equally affected.

Types

75%1) Senile Cortical in location, in 75% of cases.
25%2) Senile Nuclear in location, in 25% of cases.
Cortico-nuclear3) Senile Cortico-nuclear cataract: They may both occur at the same time.
