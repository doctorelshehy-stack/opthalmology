Cornea · Keratoconus (Conical cornea)

Definition and clinical picture

Definition: It is a developmental weakness of the central part of the corneal stroma, which under the effect of the normal IOP, will bulge forwards forming a conical projection.

Clinical picture:

Age: occurs at puberty. Sex: more in females. Bilateral condition. Positive family history.
  • Onset: gradual onset.
    • Rarely acute onset or acute relapses with marked corneal edema (acute hydrops).
  • Course: slowly progressive course and tends to stop after few years.
