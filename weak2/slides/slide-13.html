Cornea · Hypopyon ulcer

Predisposing and precipitating factors

Predisposing factors (factors favoring the ulceration):

  • General: Old age or decreased resistance following fevers, D.M. or malnutrition.
  • Local: Trauma by fingernail, F.B., trichiasis or PTDs with damage to the epithelium which seems necessary as the pneumococci can't invade the intact corneal epithelium.

Precipitating factors (factors providing the invading organism):

  • Conjunctivitis.
  • Chronic dacryocystitis.
  • Scratch by the dirty fingers.
  • Blepharitis.
  • Contaminated corneal F.B.
