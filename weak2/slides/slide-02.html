Contents of this deck

01

The Cornea

Macro- and micro-anatomy, blood, nerve and the limbus, physiology and transparency — then keratitis: classification, hypopyon, dendritic, zoster and fungal ulcers, neurotrophic and exposure ulcers, keratoconus, keratoplasty and keratorefractive surgery.

02

The Conjunctiva

Histology, vascular and nerve supply — then inflammation: mucopurulent, purulent and neonatal conjunctivitis, inclusion and adenoviral forms, trachoma with its WHO grading, allergic and phlyctenular disease, and the degenerations: pterygium, pinguecula, symblepharon.

03

Cataract

Applied anatomy and physiology of the lens, congenital and developmental cataract and its six types, senile cortical and nuclear cataract through the four stages, the preoperative work-up, surgical techniques and IOL, complicated, traumatic cataract and after-cataract.

04

Refraction

Emmetropia and ametropia, myopia and its complications, hypermetropia and the five components, astigmatism and its subdivisions, aphakia, anisometropia and the calculation of reading spectacles in presbyopia.
