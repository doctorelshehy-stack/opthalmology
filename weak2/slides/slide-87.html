Cataract · Senile Cortical

3) Mature Stage of Senile Cortical Cataract

1) Incipient 2) Immature 3) Mature 4) Hypermature

Symptoms:

Marked painless drop of vision.

Signs

  • V.A: marked drop of vision (hand movement).
  • Slit lamp examination through a fully dilated pupil will show total grayish white opacification of the lens cortex.
  • Iris shadow is absent because no clear zone between the lens opacity and the iris.
