Cataract · 2) Complicated Cataract

1) Complicated Cataract to a Local Eye Disease

Definition

It is acquired lens opacity secondary to a local eye disease (usually unilateral).

Causes
Anterior segment diseasesPosterior segment diseases
1) Perforated central corneal ulcer.1) Chorioretinitis.
2) Iridocyclitis.2) Retinal detachment.
3) Glaucoma.3) Degenerative myopia.
4) Lens subluxation or dislocation.4) Retinitis pigmentosa.
5) Prolonged use of local steroids (in VKC).5) Intraocular tumors.

Pathogenesis

  • Effect of toxins in the aqueous, Disturbed lens metabolism and nutrition.
  • It usually starts at a posterior cortical-cataract due to the thin posterior capsule and absence of a posterior subcapsular epithelium.

Treatment

  • 1) If possible, we better start by correcting the local eye disorder responsible of the complicated cataract, medically or surgically.
  • 2) Treatment of the cataract is the surgical removal of the lens if indicated.
