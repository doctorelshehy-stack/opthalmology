Blood supply and nerve supply

Blood supply:

  • The cornea is avascular.
  • This is true except for the peripheral 1 mm, where the circumcorneal ciliary plexus divides into 2 layers: a superficial conjunctival and a deep episcleral.

Nerve supply:

  • From the circum-corneal ciliary plexus of nerves lying in the sclera,
  • formed by the 2 long ciliary nerves branches from the nasociliary branches from the ophthalmic branch from the Trigeminal nerve.
