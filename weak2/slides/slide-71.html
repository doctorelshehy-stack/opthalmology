Pterygium

Definition

Pterygium is a triangular encroachment of the conjunctiva onto the cornea.

  • It is very common in Egypt.

Etiology

Unknown; it may be related to:

  • Chronic irritation by dust, wind, fumes.
  • U.V rays which is probably the most important factor.

Symptoms

  • Discomfort & Disfigurement.
  • Diminished vision: If it encroaches on the pupillary area.
  • Diplopia: in recurrent cases due to limitation of ocular movement.

Signs

  • Site: on the nasal sides (less commonly temporal). Usually bilateral
  • Shape: Triangular and consists of:
    • Apex or head: Blunt and lies over the cornea.
    • Neck: overlies the limbus.
    • Body: lies over the sclera.
