Treatment of the uncomplicated ulcer

Topical Antibiotics:

One of two strategies is applied:

  • Dual therapy: Two antibiotics to cover both gram +ve and gram -ve organisms.
  • Monotherapy with fluoroquinolones is also effective.

Atropine sulphate 1% eye drops or ointment preparations:

It produces:

  • Paralysis of the sphincter pupilae muscle and ciliary muscle which relieve the pain resulting from their spasm.
  • Dilatation of the pupil which keeps the iris away from the lens to avoid the formation of posterior synechia.

Bilateral bandage:

  • To stop eyelid movement, so will stop the mechanical irritation of the exposed nerve endings at the ulcer; so, it cuts most of the pain.
  • As well, it will prevent the peeling of the new epithelium at the bed of the ulcer, so it helps the rapid healing of the ulcer.

Hot fomentation:

whether wet or dry compresses, have a good effect through:

  • Counter-irritant effect, thus relieving the pain.
  • Improve the circulation, so wash the toxins, and brings immunoglobulins, PMNL, and nutrition for the healing process.
