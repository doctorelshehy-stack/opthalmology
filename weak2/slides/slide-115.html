Refraction · Astigmatism

Accommodation & the clinical picture of astigmatism

Role of accommodation

  • Accommodation is exerted simultaneously, symmetrically & circumferentially.
  • So; if it corrects one meridian, the other will be worsened.
  • This means that all types of astigmatism cannot be corrected with accommodation.

Clinical Picture — Symptoms

  1. Defective vision:
    • Blurred vision.
    • Running and /or dropped letters, and tailed points.
    • Uniocular diplopia (may occur in marked degree of astigmatism).
  2. Accommodative asthenopia:
    • After prolonged work.
    • It's more obvious with low degrees (continuous attempt of the ciliary muscle to correct defect).
