Glaucoma · Primary Infantile Glaucoma

Etiology & Clinical Picture

Trabeculo-dysgenesis · The triad

Etiology — one or more of the following (trabeculo-dysgenesis)
  1. Failure of complete separation of the iris from the cornea due to failure of mesodermal atrophy.
  2. Abnormal anterior insertion of the iris or longitudinal bundle of the ciliary muscle into the trabecular meshwork.
  3. Absence of canal of Schlemm.
Clinical picture — Symptoms

Usually reported by the parents as triad of:

Lacrimation Photophobia Blepharospasm
  • Due to stretching of the ciliary nerves by ↑ IOP & corneal edema.
  • The symptoms later increase in intensity with more corneal edema.
