Strabismus · Latent Squint

Clinical Picture of Latent Squint

Symptoms

  • Compensated heterophoria: No symptoms.
  • Decompensated heterophoria: Symptoms occur after fatigue or general ill-health:
    • a) Muscular asthenopia (eye strain): after prolonged close work.
    • b) Blurring and running of letters.

Signs

  • No ocular deviation: If binocular vision is maintained.
  • Ocular deviation: If binocular vision is not maintained (latent squint becomes manifest squint) [ phoria tropia].
Compensated heterophoria keeps the eyes straight; when binocular vision breaks down, the phoria becomes a tropia.
