2) Functions of the Ciliary Body & 3) Functions of the Choroid

Functions of the ciliary body

1) Function of the ciliary muscle:

  • Circular fibers: Accommodation.
  • Longitudinal fibers: Help drainage of aqueous humour.

2) Function of the ciliary processes:

  • Formation of aqueous humour.

Functions of the choroids

  • Nourishment of the outer five layers of the underlying retina.
  • Dark coating for the interior of the eye which increases efficiency.

The choroid is the vascular coat of the posterior uveal tract — it feeds the outer five retinal layers and lines the interior of the globe with pigment.

N.B.: The choroid contains no pain nerve endings and so it is insensitive to pain.
