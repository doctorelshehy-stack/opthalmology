Classification of Strabismus & the Visual / Optic Axes

Classification
  • 1) Apparent squint (false or pseudo-squint).
  • 2) True squint:
    • A. Latent squint (heterophoria).
    • B. Manifest squint: a) Paralytic squint (non-comitant/ incomitant). b) Concomitant squint (most important).
TermDefinition
Nodal pointIt is a point behind the posterior pole of the lens through which light rays undergo no refraction.
Visual axisIt is the line connecting the macular and the object of regard passing through the nodal point.
Optic axisIt is the line connecting the center of the cornea, lens and retina.
