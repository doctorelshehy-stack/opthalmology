Perimeter, Synoptophore & Prism Bar Tests

2) Perimeter test (Javal's method)
3) Synoptophore (major amblyoscope): most accurate method
  1. The patient looks through the 2 adjustable tubes at 2 pictures at the end of the tubes.
  2. The angle between the 2 tubes is changed until the pictures are superimposed.
  3. The angle between the 2 tubes is equal to the angle of squint.
4) Prism bar and cover test: Prism of increasing powers can be placed on either the fixing or squinting eye until the corneal reflex is centered in both eyes.
