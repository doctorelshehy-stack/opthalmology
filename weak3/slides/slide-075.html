DD of panophthalmitis

  • 1) Endophthalmitis.
  • 2) Orbital cellulites
  • 3) Cavernous sinus thrombosis.

Treatment: Evisceration. Panophthalmitis is distinguished from endophthalmitis by the involvement of the outer coat (cornea and sclera), Tenon's capsule and the soft orbital tissues — with proptosis and limitation of all ocular movements.
