Retina · Artery occlusion

CRAO: late signs & differential diagnosis

Signs 2) Late CRAO
  • Retina is transparent and atrophic (due to acellular scar of inner retinal layer).
  • Optic disc is pale due to consecutive optic atrophy.
  • Some eyes develop neovascularization of iris, angle and retina.
Differential diagnosis
  • Causes of sudden loss of vision.
  • Causes of cherry red spot:
  • a) Commotio retinae.
  • b) Amaurotic family idiocy.
  • c) Macular hole with RD.
  • d) Quinine poisoning.
