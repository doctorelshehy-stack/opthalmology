Glaucoma · Primary Open Angle Glaucoma

Clinical Picture

Symptoms · Signs

Symptoms
  1. Asymptomatic.
  2. Mild symptoms as:
    • Headache.
    • Early presbyopia (pressure on ciliary muscles).
    • Night blindness due to peripheral filed defects.
    • Visual filed defect or complete loss of vision in one eye.
Signs

1) ↑ IOP

2) Optic disc cupping

3) Field defects

4) Open angle by Gonioscopy
