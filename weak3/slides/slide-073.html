Uveitis · Panophthalmitis

Panophthalmitis: Definition & Etiology

Definition: It is acute suppurative inflammation of the globe which involves all the ocular tissues including the outer coat (cornea and sclera), Tenon's capsule and the soft orbital tissues.

Etiology

Exogenous infection

  • Usually leads to panophthalmitis because of the large number of virulent organisms.

Endogenous infection

  • Usually leads to endophthalmitis because the organisms are few and attenuated by the circulating antibodies.

All ocular tissues are involved: outer coat (cornea and sclera), Tenon's capsule and the soft orbital tissues.
