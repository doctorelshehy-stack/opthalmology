Visual Field Changes (continued)

Central 5–6 · Peripheral field

  • 5) Ronne nasal step defect: due to asymmetric involvement of the arcuate nerve bundles above and below the fixation point, then join each other in a horizontal line centrally or peripherally masking what is called "Nasal step".
  • 6) Temporal wedge defect: Unusual nasal narrowing of new retinal rim.
B) Peripheral field changes
  1. Nasal contraction.
  2. Concentric contraction.
  3. Finally central and peripheral changes meet → tubular field (+ / - temporal island).
The macular area is lately affected in POAG thus V.A is not a measure of the severity of the disease.
Central visual field changes occur early in the course of the disease.
