Proliferative DR & diabetic maculopathy

3) Proliferative diabetic retinopathy (PDR)
  • Neovessels at the disc (NVD).
  • Neovessels elsewhere (NVE).
  • Preretinal and vitreous hemorrhage.
  • Fibrous tissue proliferation.
  • Tractional retinal detachment (TRD).
4) Diabetic maculopathy
  • It means involvement of the fovea by edema and hard exudates or ischemia.
  • It may be focal, diffuse, ischemic or mixed.
It is called clinically significant macular edema (CSME): if the edema or exudates are 500μ from the fovea or retinal edema 1500μ area within 1500 μ of the center of the fovea.
