Strabismus · Latent Squint

A. Latent Squint (Heterophoria)

Definition

Ocular deviation with abnormal direction of the visual axes of both eyes relative to each other when the binocular vision is dissociated as by fatigue or covering one eye.

Etiology
  • 1) Uncorrected errors of refraction:
    • a) Hypermetropia: Leads to esophoria due to excessive accommodation.
    • b) Myopia: Leads to exophoria.
  • 2) Weakness of one or more of the extraocular muscles.
Types of heterophoria
TypeDeviation
EsophoriaTendency of the eye to deviate in.
ExophoriaTendency of the eye to deviate out.
HyperphoriaTendency of the eye to deviate up.
HypophoriaTendency of the eye to deviate down.
Latent squint is true squint that appears only when binocular vision is dissociated — by fatigue or by covering one eye.
