4) Measurement of the Angle of Squint: Corneal Reflex (Hirschberg)

1) Corneal reflex (Hirschberg test) (Fig. 11-14)

The patient is asked to look at a source of light at a distance of 0.5 meter, and the position of the corneal reflex is noted:

  • 1) Central corneal reflex: In the fixing eye.
  • 2) Eccentric corneal reflex: In the squinting eye:
    • It is situated on the temporal side of the cornea in convergent squint and on the nasal side of the cornea in divergent squint.
    • Its position indicates the angle of squint as follows.
    • (Each 1mm = 7°): pupil = 4mm.
