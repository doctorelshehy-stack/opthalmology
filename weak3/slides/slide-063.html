Uveitis · Iridocyclitis

Complications: Occlusio-pupillae & Cyclitic Membrane

1c) Occlusio-pupillae

  • Occlusion of the pupil by organized exudates (membrane) which leads to secondary glaucoma.

In occlusio-pupillae the whole pupil is closed by an organized exudate membrane; in seclusio-pupillae only the pupillary border is adherent to the lens (ring synechia).

2) Cyclitic membrane

  • Definition: Organized exudates behind the lens.
  • Cause: Cyclitis.
  • Clinically: Pseudo-glioma with whitish pupil.
  • Complications: Its contraction leads to severe pain and retinal detachment.
