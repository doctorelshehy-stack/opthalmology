Glaucoma · Primary Infantile Glaucoma

Diagnosis & Complications

Corneal diameter · Gonioscopy · IOP

Diagnosis
  1. Measurement of the corneal diameter: If more than 11mm considered suspicious.
  2. Gonioscopy.
  3. Measurement of the IOP.
Complications
1) Impairment of vision

Due to optic atrophy & corneal opacities with:

  • Amblyopia in unilateral cases.
  • Strabismus and nystagmus in bilateral cases.
2) Lens
  • Complicated cataract.
  • Subluxated lens.
