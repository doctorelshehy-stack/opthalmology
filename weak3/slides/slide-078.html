Anatomy of Extraocular Muscles — Origin & Insertion

A team of 6 extraocular muscles (EOMs) controls the movements of each eye.

Origin
  • 1) 4 recti muscles: From the corresponding areas of the annulus of Zinn (tendinous ring) at the apex of the orbit around the optic foramen and medial part of superior orbital fissure (SOF).
  • 2) 2 oblique muscles:
    • a) Superior oblique muscle: At the apex of the orbit, above and medial to the annulus of Zinn.
    • b) Inferior oblique muscle: Shallow depression in the floor of the orbit lateral to the opening of the nasolacrimal duct.

Insertion

The six extraocular muscles are inserted in the sclera:

1) 4 recti: Inserted in the sclera anterior to the equator of the eyeball (by a flat tendon about 10 mm in breadth) at a variable distance from the limbus (Fig.11-1).

2) Oblique muscles: Inserted in the sclera behind the equator of the eyeball (Fig. 11-2):
