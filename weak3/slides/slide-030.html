A. Latent Stage — asymptomatic phase of PACG
  • By slit lamp: Very shallow anterior chamber and close proximity of the peripheral iris to the cornea.
  • Diagnosis: By gonioscopy grade 1 or 2.
  • Treatment: Laser peripheral iridotomy to decrease risk of ACG.
  • Fate: Remain normal or change to other stages.
