Uveitis · Choroid

3) Anatomy of the Choroid

Gross anatomy

  • It is the posterior part of the uveal tract.
  • It extends from the ciliary body to the optic nerve.

Minute anatomy

  • 1) Suprachoroidal lamina: A delicate lamellae passing from the choroid to the sclera.
  • 2) Vascular layers: Outer large vessels, Middle small vessels & Inner choriocapillaris.
  • 3) Bruch's membrane: Elastic membrane in contact with the retina.

Blood supply

  • Arteries:
    • Posterior part: Short posterior ciliary arteries.
    • Anterior part: As the ciliary body.
  • Veins: 4 Vortex veins.
