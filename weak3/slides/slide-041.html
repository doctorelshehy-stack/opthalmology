6) Glaucoma Associated with ocular trauma
  • Blunt trauma e.g. angle recession glaucoma.
  • Corneal laceration and iris incarceration.
  • Chemical burns e.g. 2ry glaucoma in iris burn which may cause trabecular damage and/or scarring of episcleral veins.
  • Hyphema.
  • Corneal ulcers and iritis.
  • Lens dislocation
  • Iridocyclitis by lens matter.
7) Steroid – induced glaucoma
  • In certain susceptible individuals, prolonged use of topical steroids might lead to 2ry glaucoma that looks like POAG.
  • The mechanism of which is not quite clear, but it may be due to mucopolysaccharide deposition in the TM.
  • Treatment: Cessation of steroid.
