Strabismus · Paralytic Squint

Limitation of Movement & Binocular Diplopia

2) Limitation of movement

Occurs in the same direction of action of the paralyzed muscle (e.g. outwards in LR paralysis).

3) Binocular diplopia — definition

The patient sees double vision by the use of both eyes.

Cause: the two retinal images fall on non-corresponding points of the retina.

Characters of binocular diplopia
  • 1) It disappears when one eye is covered:
    • Unlike uniocular diplopia which disappears only when the affected eye is covered.
  • 2) Two different images are seen:
    • a) True image: Clear as it is seen by the fovea of the normal eye.
    • b) False image: Blurred as it seen by a point outside the fovea of the paralyzed eye.
The false image is blurred because it is formed outside the fovea of the paralyzed eye; the true image is clear because it is formed on the fovea of the normal eye.
