Glaucoma · Primary Open Angle Glaucoma

1) High IOP & How to Confirm the Diagnosis

Rise of IOP
  • Rise of IOP above 21 mmHg in the presence of open angle is suspicious.
  • However normal IOP does not exclude Normal or low-tension glaucoma.
  • Thus, to be sure of the diagnosis:
  1. The difference in tension of both eyes should not exceed 4 mm Hg. A higher difference is suggestive.
  2. Diurnal variation should not exceed 4 mm Hg. In hospital the IOP is measured every 4 hours for 24 hours.
  3. Measurement of the corneal thickness.
  4. Tonography:
    • Electronic Schiotz tonometer is continuously placed over the cornea for 4 min.
    • The IOP is measured every 15 sec, and a curve is drown to calculate aqueous outflow.
