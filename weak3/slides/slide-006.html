Functions of the Aqueous & the Aqueous Flow

Functions
  1. It regulates and maintains IOP.
  2. Nutrition of avascular structures as the cornea and lens.
  3. One of the refractive media (RI = 1.33).
  4. It brings antibodies (plasmoid aqueous) in cases of intra ocular inflammation (secondary aqueous).
