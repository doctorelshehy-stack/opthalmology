Glaucoma · Treatment of POAG

B) Systemic Treatment & 2) Laser Trabeculoplasty

Acetazolamide · YAG / Argon laser

B) Systemic treatment — CAI: Acetazolamide

Action: Decrease aqueous production.

Side Effect:

  • Hypokalemia (most serious – oral supplement of potassium is necessary).
  • Paresthesia of fingers and toes.
  • Leucopenia, aplastic anemia, anorexia, mental depression and weight loss.

Should be used be temporary in emergency.

2) Laser Trabeculoplasty
  • Indicated in POAG with moderately elevated IOP inspite of maximally tolerated medical treatment.
  • Either YAG or Argon laser beam is directed to the TM one or two rows for 180°.
  • This procedure increases the aqueous out flow, but the exact mechanism is not clear.
Target IOP Medical for life 180° of the TM
