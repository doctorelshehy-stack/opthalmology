Diabetes mellitus

Definition & characteristics
  • Diabetes mellitus (DM) is a common metabolic disorder.
  • Characterized by:
    • Sustained hyperglycemia secondary to lack.
    • Diminished efficacy or both of endogenous insulin.
Classification (WHO) of DM
  1. Type I: Juvenile onset, Insulin dependent diabetes mellitus (IDDM), it is due to insulin deficiency.
  2. Type II: Adult onset, Non-insulin dependent diabetes (NIDDM), it is due to insulin resistance.
Effects of diabetes on the eye
  1. Eye lids’ infection, recurrent styes and blepharitis.
  2. Xanthelasma.
  3. Recurrent corneal erosions.
  4. Rubeosis iridis.
  5. Specific complicated cataract.
  6. Presenile, early onset senile cataract.
  7. Change of refraction due to effect of DM on the lens:
    • Hyperglycemia causes index myopia.
    • Hypoglycemia causes index hypermetropia.
  8. Optic neuropathy.
  9. Extraocular muscle palsy due to neuropathy of III , ІV, ІIX.
  10. Diabetic retinopathy.
