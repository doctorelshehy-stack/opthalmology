Uveitis · Iridocyclitis

Signs: Eye Lids, Conjunctiva & Cornea

Sign 1

Eye lids

Edema and redness (due to congestion).

Sign 2

Conjunctiva

Ciliary injection.

Sign 3 — Cornea

  • Keratic precipitates (KPs): Are depositions of inflammatory cells on the back of the cornea, usually in the form of triangle with the base down due to gravity.
  • Slight haziness: Due to corneal edema and KPs.
