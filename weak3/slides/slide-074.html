Uveitis · Panophthalmitis

Panophthalmitis: Clinical Picture

Symptoms

As in endophthalmitis but more severe.

Signs

Sign 1

Signs of endophthalmitis but:

  • Severer.
  • Ring abscess of the cornea.

Sign 2

Signs of Tenon's capsule affection

  • Proptosis.
  • Limitation of all ocular movements.
N.B.: There is no proptosis or limitation of ocular movements in Endophthalmitis because the Tenon's capsule is not affected.
