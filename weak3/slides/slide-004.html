Gonioscopic Appearance of the Angle

Structures seen & not seen

Seen on gonioscopy — 1 to 5
  • 1) Schwalbe's line (SL): white line corresponding to the end of Descemet's membrane.
  • 2) Trabecular meshwork (TM): a meshwork of sponge like bands with holes that extends from SL to scleral spur.
  • 3) Scleral spur (SS): scleral ridge projected into the anterior chamber & give origin to ciliary muscle.
  • 4) Ciliary body (CB) anterior surface.
  • 5) Root of the iris.
Not seen on gonioscopy — 6 to 7

The following structures are not seen on gonioscopy:

  • 6) Schlemm's canal: circular canal inside the limbus, it appears oval in cut section.
  • 7) Aqueous veins: drain the aqueous from the Schlemm's canal to the episcleral veins >> Ant ciliary veins >>> venous circulation.
SL → TM → SS → CB → iris root: the five structures listed in order from the end of Descemet's membrane to the root of the iris.
