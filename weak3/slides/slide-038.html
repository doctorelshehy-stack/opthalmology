Glaucoma · Secondary Glaucoma — Lens Disorders

4) Lens Disorders (Phacogenic Causes) — Open Angle

Phacolytic · Phacoanaphylactic

1) Phacolytic glaucoma (lens protein)
  • In hypermature cataract, lens protein may escape from microscopic openings in the lens capsule that directly block the trabecular meshwork or engulfed by macrophages that swell and obstruct the angle.
  • The clinical picture like acute congestive glaucoma but with normal depth of A. Ch, open angle and reaction in A. Ch.
  • Treatment by lens extraction, beta blocker and steroids.
2) Phacoanaphylactic Glaucoma (phacoantigenic)
  • After lens trauma or after ECCE with rupture of the lens capsule.
  • The lens protein causes phacoanaphylactic reaction (iritis) within the eye with associated edema of the trabecular mesh work and the proteins will be deposited in the TM.
  • Treatment is by steroid and anti-glaucoma medications.
