Glaucoma · Primary Angle Closure Glaucoma

D. Chronic Stage of Angle Closure Glaucoma

It follows acute or intermittent phases.

Gonioscopy: shows variable degree of angle closure and PAS.
Clinical picture
  • Moderate rise of IOP with symptoms and signs as POAG.
  • Acute attacks may occur on top of chronic stage.
  • If left untreated, progressive visual field occur with extensive glaucomatous cupping.
Treatment: by medical treatment and filtering surgery (trabeculectomy) with prophylactic iridotomy to the other eye.
