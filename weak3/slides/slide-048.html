Uveitis · Ciliary Body

2) Anatomy of the Ciliary Body — Minute Anatomy

A. Ciliary muscle — 3 parts

  • 1) Longitudinal fibers:
    • Site: Outermost part of the ciliary body.
    • Attachments: It extends from the scleral spur to the suprachoroidal lamina.
  • 2) Circular fibers:
    • Site: The inner anterior part of the ciliary body.
    • Attachment: Form a ring around the ciliary process.
  • 3) Oblique fibers: Connects the longitudinal and circular fibers.

B & C — processes and epithelium

  • B. Ciliary processes:
    • 70-80 in number.
    • Each process is formed of blood vessels covered by ciliary epithelium.
  • C. Epithelium:
    • Pigmented epithelium: Single outer layer of flat cells.
    • Non-Pigmented epithelium: Single inner layer of cubical cells.

Blood & nerve supply

  • Arteries: Circulus arteriosus major and arteries forming it.
  • Veins: 4 vortex veins & Anterior ciliary veins (carry blood from the ciliary muscle).
  • Motor: Parasympathetic fibers to the ciliary muscle (via the 3rd cranial nerve).
  • Sensory: 2 Long ciliary nerves.
N.B.: Ora serrata is a dark serrated line in between the choroid and ciliary body.
