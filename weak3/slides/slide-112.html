Strabismus · Concomitant Squint

Surgical Treatment: Indications & Aim

Indications
  1. Residual angle of squint of more than 10° after correction of the refractive error.
  2. Squint with no error of refraction, non – accommodative esotropia.
  3. Neglected squint above the age of 10 years for cosmetic purpose only.
  4. Most cases of exotropia.
Surgery is indicated after the refractive error has been corrected and the non-surgical lines of treatment have been considered.
Aim of surgery
1) Strengthening a muscle
  • a) Resection (shortens the muscle---increase power).
  • b) Advancement (carrying the insertion anterior).
  • c) Plication: Tightening its fibers.
2) Weakening a muscle
  • Recession (carrying the insertion backward).
