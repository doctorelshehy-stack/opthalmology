2) Primary Angle Closure Glaucoma (PACG)

Definition

It is a primary glaucoma in which the IOP is elevated (usually acute) as a result of partial or complete occlusion of the angle of anterior chamber by iris periphery.

Incidence
  • Bilateral disease (one eye is affected before the other).
  • Usually in females above 40 years (particular neurotic).
  • Mydriasis, hypermetropia (small eyes) and old age (increase thickness of the lens) may be predisposing factors.
