Pathogenesis: Mechanism of Angle Closure

Pupillary block · Plateau iris

A. Pupillary block (Iris bombe)

  • Most common where shallow anterior chamber especially in hypermetropia.
  • Relatively anterior location of the iris – lens diaphragm.
  • Increase size of the lens by age.
  • Slight apposition of the iris to the lens leads relative papillary block that traps aqueous in posterior chamber.
  • Dilation of the pupil leads to relaxation of the iris and the accumulated aqueous push the iris root to form iris bombe and angle closure.

B. Plateau iris (iris crowding) (Fig. 9-10)

  • Flat central iris with peripheral dragging at the angle caused by anterior positioned ciliary process that force the peripheral iris anteriorly.
