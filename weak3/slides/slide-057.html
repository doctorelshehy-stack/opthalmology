Uveitis · Iridocyclitis

Clinical Picture — Symptoms: 1) Neuralgic Pain

Causes and characters of the neuralgic pain

Causes Characters
a) Irritation of sensory nerves of the iris and ciliary body. a) Dull ache in the eye (neuralgic).
b) Spasm of sphincter pupillae muscle and ciliary muscle. b) Severe at night.
c) Increased tension (secondary glaucoma). c) Referred as headache to the eyebrow (along the branches of the 5th nerve).

The three characters of the pain correspond to the three causes: irritation of the sensory nerves of the iris and ciliary body, spasm of the sphincter pupillae and ciliary muscles, and increased tension (secondary glaucoma).
