Diagnosis of Latent Squint: Cover (Screen) Test

Diagnosis of latent squint depends on dissociation of both eyes i.e. to abolish binocular vision.
1) Cover (screen) test (Fig.11-4) — Aim

Dissociation of binocular vision by covering one eye to observe the movement of the covered eye only, on removing the cover.

Method
  1. The patient is asked to fix an object (as the examiner's finger) at 50 cm.
  2. One eye is covered by a card and observed under the cover.
  3. If latent squint is present, the eye behind the cover will move and on removing the cover, it will return to its original position.
