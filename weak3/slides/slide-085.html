Angle Alpha & Apparent Squint (Pseudo-strabismus)

Angle alpha

It is the angle between the visual axis and optic axis at the nodal point.

  • Normally, it is +ve, 2° to 5°.
  • Emmetrope ---5° and +ve (i.e visual axis cuts optic axis nasal to the optic axis).
  • In hypermetrope---- it is large +ve.
  • In high myope------It is small and even –ve.
1) Apparent Squint (Pseudo-strabismus)

Definition: Appearance of ocular deviation but the visual axes of both eyes are normally directed.

Types and etiology: Decrease in the visible white portion of the sclera:

  • 1) Apparent convergent squint as in: Epicanthus: Skin fold covering the inner canthus, congenital, racial.
  • 2) Apparent divergent squint as in: High hypermetropia (due to large +ve angle alpha).
