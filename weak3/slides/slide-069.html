Uveitis · Iridocyclitis

C. Treatment of the Complications (4-6)

Complication 4

Complicated cataract

  • Cataract extraction when the eye becomes quiet and with good projection.

Complication 5

Endophthalmitis

  • Medical treatment: with subconjunctival and systemic antibiotics.
  • Evisceration: If the medical treatment fails.

Complication 6

Painful atrophic globe

  • Enucleation or retrobulbar injection of alcohol.

Operations on the quiet eye — cataract extraction, iridectomy and lens extraction — are deferred until the eye becomes quiet and there is good projection.
