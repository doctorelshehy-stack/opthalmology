Endophthalmitis: Definition & Clinical Picture

Definition

  • It is acute suppurative inflammation inside the globe.
  • Acute postoperative sterile endophthalmitis is due to:
    • a) Retained foreign bodies as sponges or powder from gloves.
    • b) Too much manipulation as in vitrectomy.

Symptoms

  • Painful drop of vision with red eye ± General constitutional symptoms (fever, nausea, etc.).

Signs

  1. Eye lids: edema and redness.
  2. Conjunctiva: Chemosis and hyperemia (with ciliary and conjunctival injection).
  3. Cornea: Hazy with keratic precipitates.
  4. Anterior chamber: Hypopyon.
  5. Fundus: Yellowish reflex (amaurotic cat's eye) due to pus in the vitreous.
N.B.: The outer coat of the globe (cornea and sclera) and Tenon's capsule are not affected in endophthalmitis.
