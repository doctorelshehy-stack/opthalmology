True exfoliation

Due to exposure to infra-red rays → exfoliation of lens capsule → deposited at the angle.

Pseudo-exfoliation glaucoma

Abnormal deposits of unknown origin are seen over:

  • The lens surface.
  • Ciliary processes.
  • Zonules.
  • Posterior surface of the iris.
  • Trabecular meshwork.

That close its fenestrations leading to rise of IOP.
