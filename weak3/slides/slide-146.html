Retina · Retinal detachment

I. Rhegmatogenous retinal detachment

Mechanism
  • It is caused by retinal breaks that allow liquefied vitreous to gain access to the subretinal space causing progressive RD.
  • Dynamic vitreoretinal traction which leads to retinal tears.
  • Peripheral retinal degeneration which leads to formation of retinal hole.
Risk factors
  1. High myopia.
  2. Cataract surgery complicated by vitreous loss.
  3. Recent severe trauma.
  4. History of RD in the fellow eye.
  5. Family history of RD.
