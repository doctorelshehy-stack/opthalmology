Uveitis · Iridocyclitis

C. Treatment of the Complications (1-3)

1
Secondary glaucoma

Early (due to plasmoid aqueous, exudates or hypopyon): Medical treatment of iridocyclitis (atropine cannot be stopped); Diamox is given; Paracentesis only for severe increasing intraocular pressure (as it may cause peripheral anterior synechiae).

Late (due to Seclusio-pupillae with iris bombe or due to peripheral anterior synechiae): Filtering operation when the eye becomes quiet.

2
Seclusio-Pupillae (ring synechia) with iris bombe

Without peripheral anterior synechiae: Laser iridotomy or peripheral iridectomy.

With peripheral anterior synechiae: Filtering operation.

3
Total posterior synechiae

Iridectomy and lens extraction when eye becomes quiet and with good projection.
