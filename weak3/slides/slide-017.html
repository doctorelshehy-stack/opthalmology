Glaucoma · Primary Infantile Glaucoma

Management

Always surgical — the earlier the better

Always surgical: the earlier the interference the better the prognosis. A) Medical treatment: used preoperatively to ↓ IOP and corneal edema.
B) Surgical treatment

1) Corneal diameter < 13 mm & clear cornea

a) Goniotomy: is the standard operation and done in early case with clear cornea where a goniotomy knife is introduced from the limbus and passed across the anterior chamber to the angle on the opposite side and then 1/3 of the angle circumference is incised to cut the abnormal mesodermal membrane.

b) Trabeculotomy: it is indicated in failure of repeated goniotomy or advanced cases with hazy cornea — a trabeculotome is introduced into Schlemm canal and rotated into the anterior chamber to expose S.C to the aqueous in the anterior chamber.

2) Corneal diameter > 13 mm & failure of goniotomy or trabeculotomy (S.C is absent or fibrosed)

  • a) Subscleral trabeculectomy (see later).
  • b) Valve implantation (Ahmed valve).
  • c) Cyclodestructive procedure in advanced cases.
