2) Choroiditis — How Posterior Uveitis Differs

N.B.: Posterior uveitis (choroiditis) differs from anterior uveitis (iridocyclitis) in being:
Difference Posterior uveitis (choroiditis) vs anterior uveitis (iridocyclitis)
1) Painless Choroiditis is painless — the choroid contains no pain nerve endings.
2) Vision No marked visual loss (except if the central area is affected).
3) Tension Secondary glaucoma is not common.
4) Drugs Corticosteroids are important than atropine and are given by the periocular and systemic route.
5) Severe cases Cytotoxic drugs and other immunosuppressants may be used in severe sight-threatening cases.
