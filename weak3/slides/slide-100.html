Strabismus · Paralytic Squint

Treatment of Paralytic Squint

1) Treat the cause

Either by internist or neurologist, diabetes, hypertension, etc.

2) Occlusion of one eye

To avoid diplopia until the muscle regains its function with recovery within 6 months:

  • a) Occlusion of the paralyzed eye.
  • b) Alternate occlusion with orthoptic exercises is better.
3) Surgical treatment

If no recovery occurs after 6 months.

4) Relieving prisms
  • Transmits the image to the fovea with keeping the deviation.
  • The base of the prism is against the direction of strabismus.
  • The prismatic power is divided equally between both eyes.

Indication:

  • During the period of observation to avoid diplopia.
  • If the patient refused or was unfit for surgery.
The muscle is given time to recover — if recovery does not occur after 6 months, surgical treatment is indicated.
