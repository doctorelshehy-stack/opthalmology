Uveitis: Definition & Classification

Definition: Uveitis is inflammation of the uveal tract.

Classification According to
Anatomical
  • Which part of uvea is affected by inflammation.
Clinical
  • Mode of onset and course of inflammation.
  • Uveitis is divided into acute and chronic uveitis.
Etiological
  • Cause of inflammation.
  • Uveitis can be divided into infected and noninfected.
Pathological
  • Pathology present.
  • Uveitis is divided into granulomatous and non-granulomatous.

Anatomical types

Type 1Anterior uveitis(iridocyclitis)
Type 2Posterior uveitis(choroiditis)
Type 3Panuveitis(iridocyclitis and choroiditis)
