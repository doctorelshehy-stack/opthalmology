Definition
  • It is pressure at which the eye functioning normally.
  • It is approximately 16 mmHg with a standard deviation of 3 mmHg (Range of normal IOP is 10-21 mmHg).
  • It is a very significant risk factor for developing glaucoma.
Diurnal variation
  • Normally the IOP is highest in the morning and goes to minimum in the evening (the difference is 2-3 mmHg).
  • This is because of extra ocular muscle massage and venous stagnation during sleep (↓aqueous outflow).
However, there is no clear line between safe and unsafe IOP (some eyes underwent severe damage at IOP of 18mmHg, and other tolerate IOP in the high twenties).
