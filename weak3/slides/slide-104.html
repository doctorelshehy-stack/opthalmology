Strabismus · Concomitant Squint

Clinical Picture of Concomitant Squint

Symptoms
  • Ocular deviation: With bad cosmetic appearance.
  • Defective vision: In unilateral squint (poor vision in the squinting eye).
Signs
  • Decreased visual acuity in unilateral squint due to either:
    • Stimulus deprivation amblyopia.
    • Or Strabismic amblyopia.
  • Ocular deviation: With a constant angle of squint.
N.B.: There is no diplopia and no limitation of movements (unlike paralytic squint).
  • 1) No diplopia as there is no binocular vision (due to suppression of the blurred image of squinting eye by the brain with uniocular vision).
  • 2) No limitation of movements as there is no muscle paralysis or paresis.
