Strabismus · Manifest Squint

a) Paralytic Squint (Incomitant Strabismus)

Definition

Ocular deviation with abnormal direction of the visual axes of both eyes relative to each other due to paralysis (complete loss of muscle function) or paresis (partial loss) of one or more of the extraocular muscles, in which the angle of squint is variable in different directions of gaze.

Etiology

Any motor obstacle affecting:

  • The ocular motor nuclei.
  • Ocular motor nerves.
  • Or extraocular muscles.

(i.e. a lower motor neuron lesion as the supranuclear lesions lead to paralysis of conjugate movements and not individual muscle paralysis).

Symptoms
  1. Diplopia: In all or certain directions of gaze.
  2. Ocular deviation with limitation of movements of the affected eye: with bad cosmetic appearance.
  3. Ocular torticollis (head rotation and or tilt): A compensatory head posture (CHP) which is adopted by the patient to avoid diplopia and to maintain binocular single vision (common in congenital cases).
  4. Vertigo, dizziness, uncertain gait, nausea and vomiting: Are due to diplopia or false projection of objects.
