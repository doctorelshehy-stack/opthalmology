Uveitis · Physiology

1) Functions of the Iris

  1. Functions of the pupil:
    • It regulates the amount of light entering the eye.
    • It cuts off the peripheral rays to:
    • Increase the depth of focus when looking at a near object.
    • Increase the sharpness of the retinal image.
  2. Actions of the plain muscles of the iris:
    • Sphincter pupillae muscle: Miosis.
    • Dilator pupillae muscle: Mydriasis.
  3. Function of the iris crypts: Aqueous drainage.
  4. Function of the anterior endothelium: Phagocytosis.
