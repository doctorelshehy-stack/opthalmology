Uveitis · Ciliary Body

2) Anatomy of the Ciliary Body — Gross Anatomy

a) Site

  • It is the intermediate part of the uveal tract.
  • Extends from the iris to the choroid.

b) Shape

  • Is triangular in antero-posterior section:
    • 1) The base (anterior surface): Is directed anteriorly to be continuous with: the iris root centrally, the trabecular meshwork peripherally.
    • 2) The apexes directed posteriorly to be continuous with the choroid.

c) External surface

  • Is separated from the sclera by the supraciliary space.

d) Internal surface

  • Faces the vitreous, gives origin to the zonules and is divided into:
    • 1) Pars plicata (anterior part): Contains 70-80 ciliary processes.
    • 2) Pars plana (posterior part): Is smooth and it ends at the ora serrata.

The base of the triangle is continuous with the iris root centrally and the trabecular meshwork peripherally; the apexes are continuous with the choroid.
