Glaucoma · Acquired Glaucoma — Secondary Type

Secondary Glaucoma (1)

Systemic disease · Local causes

It is type of glaucoma arising secondary to systemic or local disease.

A. Systemic disease

It results from rise of pressure in the episcleral veins results in impaired aqueous drainage e.g. dysthyroid ophthalmopathy and superior venacaval obstruction.

B. 1) Corneal disorders
  • Open angle: corneal ulcer and iritis → plasmoid aqueous.
  • Closed angle: leucoma adherent and anterior staphyloma → PAS
B) Local causes — the seven groups
1) Corneal disorders 2) Anterior chamber disorders 3) Iris and C.B. disorders 4) Lens disorders (phacogenic) 5) Intraocular hemorrhage 6) Ocular trauma 7) Steroid-induced glaucoma
