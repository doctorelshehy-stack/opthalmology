Glaucoma · Secondary Glaucoma — Lens Disorders

4) Lens Disorders — Closed Angle

Phacomorphic · Pupillary block

Closed angle
1) Phacomorphic glaucoma (intumescent cataract)
  • During intumescent stage of senile cataract, the lens size increase leading to: shallow anterior chamber and pupillary block leading to: rise of IOP simulating acute angle closure glaucoma.
  • Treatment is just lens extraction.
2) Pupillary block

Due to anterior and posterior dislocation of the lens.

Shallow A.C. Pupillary block ↑ IOP
Phacomorphic = lens swelling in intumescent senile cataract → shallow anterior chamber → pupillary block → rise of IOP that simulates acute angle closure glaucoma.
