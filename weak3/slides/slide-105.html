Examination of Extraocular Muscles: Inspection & Cover Test

1) Inspection
  • 1) Obvious squint: If the angle of deviation is more than 15°.
  • 2) Cause of apparent squint: To be excluded.
2) Cover (screen) test — 1) Aim

To differentiate between:

  • a) Apparent, latent and manifest squint.
  • b) Paralytic and concomitant squint.
  • c) Unilateral and alternating concomitant squint.
  • d) Convergent and divergent concomitant squint.
2) Method
  • Ask the patient to fix an object (1st at a distant then at near).
  • The card is shifted from one eye to the other and the movements of the covered and uncovered eyes are observed.
