Clinical Features of Ocular Motor Palsies: Right 6th Nerve Palsy

1) Right 6th nerve (Right LR) palsy
  • 1) Ocular deviation:
    • a) Right eye is deviated inwards i.e. right convergent squint.
    • b) Ocular deviation is more when the right eye is turned outwards.
  • 2) Limitation of ocular movements. Of the right eye outwards.
  • 3) Binocular diplopia: Is uncrossed and is marked when the right eye is turned outwards.
  • 4) False projection: When the right eye is turned outwards.
  • 5) Face turn: To the right side.
