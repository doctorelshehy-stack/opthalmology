Primary Open Angle Glaucoma (POAG)

Chronic simple glaucoma

Definition

Bilateral, asymmetrical, non-congestive ↑ of IOP in absence of angle closure leading to optic nerve damage & visual field defect.

Incidence
  • Age: more common above 50.
  • Sex: equal.
  • Bilateral: but one eye precedes the other.
Risk factors
  • Positive Family history of glaucoma (hereditary).
  • Race: Dark races (4 times more).
  • Ocular diseases: CRVO and high myopia.
  • Systemic diseases: DM & vasospastic disorders such as migraine.
  • Ocular hypertension (↑ IOP with no manifestations of glaucoma).
Etiology: with age → sclerosis of trabecular meshwork → narrowing of spaces of Fontana → decreased aqueous outflow.
