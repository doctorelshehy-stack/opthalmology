Uveitis · Accommodation

Accommodation — Definition & Mechanism

Definition: It is the ability of the eye to change its dioptric power to see object clearly at different (far and near) distances.

Mechanism

Step 1 Circular fibers contract Contraction of the circular fibers of ciliary muscle
→
Step 2 Zonules relax Relaxation of the zonular fibers
→
Step 3 Lens becomes globular The lens becomes more spherical (globular) especially at its anterior surface
→
Step 4 Lens power rises The lens power is increased
