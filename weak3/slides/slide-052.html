Uveitis · Accommodation

Accommodation — Definition & Mechanism

Definition: It is the ability of the eye to change its dioptric power to see object clearly at different (far and near) distances.

Mechanism

1
Circular fibers contractContraction of the circular fibers of ciliary muscle.
2
Zonules relaxRelaxation of the zonular fibers.
3
Lens becomes globularThe lens becomes more spherical (globular) especially at its anterior surface.
4
Lens power risesThe lens power is increased.
