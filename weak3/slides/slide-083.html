True Squint, Apparent Squint & Orthophoria

Definition
True squint: Ocular deviation with abnormal direction of the visual axes of both eyes relative to each other (or not directed to the same object) or abnormal deviation of visual axes.
Apparent squint: Appearance of ocular deviation but the visual axes of both eyes are normally directed relative to each other (or to the same object).
N.B.: Normal directions of the visual axes of both eyes relative to each other are: a) Parallel direction: On looking to a far object. b) Converging direction: On looking to a near object.
Orthophoria
  • It is a perfect muscle balance with parallelism of the visual axes even in absence of binocular vision (as by covering one eye).
  • Both eyes are central, so, both center of pupil are fixed to that object.
Strabismus: Ocular deviation with abnormal direction of the visual axes of both eyes relative to each other — classified as apparent squint or true squint (latent and manifest).
