Retina · Artery occlusion

Retinal arterial occlusion: causes

Causes
  1. Thrombus formation following atherosclerosis.
  2. Embolism:
    • Cholesterol or fibrin-platelet from atherosclerotic plaque in carotid artery.
    • Calcific emboli from a diseased calcific heart valve.
    • Thrombus from diseased heart, following myocardial infarction.
    • Talc emboli associated with intravenous drug use.
    • Fat emboli after long bone fracture.
  3. Spasm: as in hypertension and migraine.
  4. Vasculitis: as in giant cell arteritis.
