Uncrossed vs Crossed Diplopia

3) Uncrossed (homonymous) or crossed (heteronymous) diplopia (Fig. 11-8).

FeatureUncrossed diplopiaCrossed diplopia
Where the false image falls When the false image falls on the nasal side the fovea and so will be projected on the temporal side of the paralyzed eye. When the false image falls on the temporal side of the fovea and so will be projected on the nasal side of the paralyzed eye.
Side of the paralyzed eye The false image falls on the same side of paralyzed eye (e.g. LR paralysis). The false image falls on the opposite side of the paralyzed eye (e.g. MR paralysis).
