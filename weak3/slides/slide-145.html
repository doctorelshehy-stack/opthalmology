Retinal detachment (RD): definition & classification

Definition
  • RD is separation of the sensory retina from retinal pigment epithelium (RPE).
  • The line of separation corresponds to the potential space between the neuroretina and RPE.
Classification: according to the underlying pathology
  1. Rhegmatogenous RD.
  2. Non rhegmatogenous tractional RD.
  3. Non rhegmatogenous exudative RD
