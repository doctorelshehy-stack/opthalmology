Values of the Cover Test & Ocular Movements

4) Values of the cover test
  • 1) Diagnosis of squint and DD between its types.
  • 2) Diagnosis of eccentric fixation.
  • 3) DD between:
    • 1) Refractive and muscular asthenopia.
    • 2) Uniocular and binocular diplopia.
3) Ocular movements — Methods
  • The patient is asked to follow a fixation object (as the examiner's finger) at 0.5 meter distance.
  • The ocular movements are examined in the 6 cardinal positions of gaze.
Results
  • 1) In concomitant squint: No limitation of ocular movements.
  • 2) In paralytic squint: There is limitation of ocular movements in the direction of the action of the paralyzed muscle.
