Grades of Binocular Vision

There are three grades of binocular vision.

GradeAbility
Grade I
(simultaneous perception)
  • The ability to see two dissimilar objects presented simultaneously to each eye
  • (e.g. a bird and a cage).
Grade II
(fusion)
  • The ability to superimpose 2 incomplete but similar objects to form one complete picture
  • (e.g. one rabbit with a tail and the other rabbit with a bunch of flowers and no tail).
Grade III
(stereoscopic vision)
  • The ability to appreciate depth (with three dimensions i.e. length, breadth and depth) by the superimposition of two slightly dissimilar objects
  • (e.g. 2 buckets with slightly dissimilar features).
