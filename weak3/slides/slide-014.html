Glaucoma · Primary Infantile Glaucoma

Clinical Picture: Signs (6–9)

Lens · Optic disc · Gonioscopy · Refraction

  • 6) Lens: subluxated due to limbal enlargement & weakening of zonular ligaments.
  • 7) Optic disc: Glaucomatous optic cupping is a late sign due to expansibility of the globe.
  • 8) Gonioscopy: under general anesthesia by Koeppe lens.
    • It shows flat anterior insertion of the iris into TM with the absence of angle recess and poorly developed scleral spur.
  • 9) Refraction: Axial myopia due to enlargement of the globe.
    • Usually less than expected because flattened cornea & posterior displacement of the lens.
Corneal diameter > 12 mm Haab's striae Cupping = late sign
