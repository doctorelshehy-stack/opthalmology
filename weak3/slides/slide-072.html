Uveitis · Endophthalmitis

Endophthalmitis: Complications & Treatment

Complications

1Panophthalmitis
2Pseudo-glioma
3Atrophia bulbi

Treatment

Early cases

1) Medical treatment:

  • a) Antibiotics: intravitreal, topical and systemic).
  • b) Atropine: Drops and ointment.
  • c) Corticosteroids.

2) Vitrectomy — in controlled cases.

Late cases

Evisceration.

In endophthalmitis the inflammation is confined to the inside of the globe: the cornea, sclera and Tenon's capsule are spared, so there is no proptosis and no limitation of ocular movements.
