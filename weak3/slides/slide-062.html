Uveitis · Iridocyclitis

Complications: 1) Synechiae

1a

Peripheral anterior synechiae

Angle block with adhesions which lead to secondary glaucoma.

1b

Posterior synechiae

Adhesions between the posterior surface of the iris and lens.

1b — types of posterior synechiae

  • Seclusio-pupillae (ring synechia): Adhesions between the pupillary border and lens which lead to:
    • Festooned pupil on dilatation.
    • Iris bombe and secondary glaucoma.
  • Total posterior synechia: Adhesions between the whole posterior surface of the iris and lens which occur in cyclitis and lead to secondary glaucoma.

Posterior synechiae — Festooned pupil

The iris pupillary border is pulled toward the lens in patches, so the pupil dilates in a festooned (scalloped) shape.
