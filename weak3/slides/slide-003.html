Anatomy of the angle of the anterior chamber
Definition

It is the angle between the periphery of the cornea and the root of the iris.

  • The angle of the AC can't be examined by the direct illumination.
  • We use Gonioscopy (e.g Goldman 3- mirror contact lens) + slit lamp.
Gonioscopy + slit lamp — the only way to examine the angle of the anterior chamber clinically.
