E. Absolute Glaucoma

Neglected cases — no PL vision

Definition
  • Neglected glaucoma cases with no PL vision and extensive glaucomatous cupping.
  • The IOP may be high 1st that later on may be decreased due to atrophy of the C.B. (ciliary shut down).
The eye may show
  • Degenerative corneal pannus.
  • Ciliary congestion.
  • Patches of iris atrophy.
  • Rubeosis iridis.
  • Staphyloma.
Treatment

Indicated if the eye is painful. It ranges from:

  • Medical treatment.
  • Trabeculectomy.
  • Cyclo-destructive procedures.
  • In some cases retrobulbar alcohol to destroy ciliary ganglion.
  • Or even enucleation was suggested.
