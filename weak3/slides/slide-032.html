Glaucoma · Primary Angle Closure Glaucoma

C. Acute Congestive ACG

It is caused by sudden total acute closure of the angle.

Symptoms
  • Rapid progressive decrease of vision (corneal edema and optic nerve ischemia).
  • Severe periocular pain and congestion.
  • Nausea and vomiting (vagus nerve stimulation).
Signs
  • Lid edema.
  • Ciliary congestion.
  • Hazy cornea.
  • Iris bombe and very shallow anterior chamber.
  • Semi dilated, unreactive, vertically oval pupil.
  • Gonioscopy ——— Grade O.
