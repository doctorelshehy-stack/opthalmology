Glaucoma · Aqueous Circulation

Physiology of the Aqueous

Definition
  • Aqueous humor is a transparent colorless fluid which fills the anterior and posterior chamber.
  • Its volume is 1.25 ml.
  • It is mainly secreted by the ciliary body.

It is either active or passively secreted:

Active secretion — 80%

By the non-pigmented ciliary epithelium of the ciliary process (by carbonic anhydrase enzyme).

Passive secretion — 20%

By ultrafiltration and diffusion, depending on the level of blood pressure in the ciliary capillaries.
