Causes of the rapid diminution of vision

  1. Aqueous and vitreous turbidity.
  2. Corneal edema and keratic precipitates.
  3. Toxic macular edema.
  4. Spasm of the ciliary muscle with transient myopia.
  5. Secondary glaucoma due to angle block with plasmoid aqueous and exudates.
