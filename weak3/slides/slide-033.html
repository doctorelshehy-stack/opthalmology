C. Acute Congestive ACG — Treatment

During the attack · After the attack

1) During the attack — medical treatment in the hospital
  • Sedatives.
  • Hyperosmotic agents: I.V. Acetazolamide 500 mg or mannitol 20% given as a rapid IV drip (1-2 gm / kg body weight) or oral glycerin.
  • Carbonic Anhydrase Inhibitor: Oral acetazolamide 250 mg / 6 hrs.
  • Beta blockers to decrease aqueous formation.
  • Miotics: Pilocarpine eye drop, given every 15 minutes after lowering the IOP.
2) After the attack

Once the IOP is controlled, gonioscopy is performed:

  1. Peripheral surgical iridectomy or laser iridotomy if the angle is mostly open.
  2. Trabeculectomy surgery if 50% of the angle closed or the IOP is not controlled.
  3. Prophylactic iridotomy to the other eye.
