Congenital Glaucoma (Buphthalmos)

Definition

Increase in IOP which results in damage of the optic nerve due to congenital anomalies in the angle of the anterior chamber.

1) Primary infantile glaucoma (Buphthalmus)
  • It is due to trabeculo-dysgenesis.
  • Where symptoms and signs appear between birth and 1st year.
2) Secondary infantile glaucoma

Associated with another congenital syndrome:

  • Ocular anomalies: Microphthalmos, Aniridia.
  • Marfan syndrome.
  • Sturge – Weber syndrome.
