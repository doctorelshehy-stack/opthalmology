Uveitis · Iridocyclitis

Complications 3-6: Glaucoma, Cataract, Endophthalmitis, Atrophia Bulbi

Complication 3

Secondary glaucoma

  • Early due to: Angle block by plasmoid aqueous, exudates, hypopyon or Hyphema.
  • Late due to:
    • 1) Peripheral anterior synechiae.
    • 2) Seclusio-pupillae with iris bombe.

Complication 4

Complicated cataract

  • Posterior cortical cataract on slit-lamp examination (due to diffusion of toxins as the posterior capsule of the lens is thin).

Complication 5

Endo and panophthalmitis

  • In suppurative iridocyclitis.

Complication 6

Atrophia bulbi

  • Due to atrophy of the ciliary body.
