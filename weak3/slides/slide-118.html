Retina · Anatomy

The appearance of the normal fundus

1) Direct ophthalmoscope 2) Indirect ophthalmoscope 3) Slit lamp
  • The retina is transparent showing the red color of the underlying choroid.
  • Optic disc is placed to the nasal side of the posterior pole of the eye.
    • It is 1.5 mm in diameter, pale pink, with sharp defined edge.
    • The pale depression in the center of the disc is the physiological cup.
  • Macula lutea: The dark central area 3 mm temporal to the optic disc.
  • The fovea: The depression in the center of the macula.
  • Foveola: The avascular center of the fovea.
  • Retinal vessels are transparent and show the color of blood.
  • Central retinal artery emerges from cup and divides into upper and lower branches. Each branch divides into nasal and temporal branches.
  • The veins follow arteries.
  • Artery diameter / Vein diameter = 2/3.
