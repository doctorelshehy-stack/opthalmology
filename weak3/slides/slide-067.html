Uveitis · Iridocyclitis

Local Corticosteroids & General Treatment

A. Local treatment — continued

3) Corticosteroid drugs:

  • a) Anti-inflammatory action: By decreasing the capillary permeability with diminished exudates and synechiae formation.
  • b) Anti –allergic: More effective in allergic iridocyclitis.
  • c) Fibrinolytic: By inhibiting fibroblastic reaction with diminished fibrosis and synechiae formation.
  • d) Decreased vascularity: By inhibiting new vessel formation.
  • 4) Dark glasses: For photophobia and protection.

B. General treatment

  1. Anti-infective therapy:
    • For suppurative iridocyclitis depending on the causative organism.
    • For septic foci.
  2. Systemic corticosteroid drugs for severe and resistant cases.
  3. Analgesics and non-steroidal anti-inflammatory drugs:
    • If systemic corticosteroids are contraindicated & in rheumatic iridocyclitis.
