3) Visual Field Changes — Central field (central 30°)

Due to optic nerve fibers damage and it follow its anatomical distribution: A) Central field changes (central 30°):

  1. Isolated para central scotomata: in Bjerrum area (10-20 of fixation).
  2. Seidle scotoma: Extension of the blind spot above or below in a sickle – shape.
  3. Arcuate (Bjerrum) scotoma: which arch above or below the fixation point and continuous with the blind spot.
  4. Baring of the blind spot: 1st enlargement then exclusion of the blind spot from the central field.
Bjerrum area 10-20° of fixation Seidle = sickle-shaped Arcuate = continuous with blind spot
