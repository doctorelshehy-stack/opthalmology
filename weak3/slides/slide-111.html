Strabismus · Concomitant Squint

Treatment of Concomitant Squint: Aim, Time & Lines

Aim
  • a) To build and restore binocular vision.
  • b) To improve and preserve visual acuity in squinting eye.
  • c) To restore normal appearance (cosmetic).
Time: as early as possible
  • To avoid amblyopia.
  • To allow binocular vision to develop.
Lines of treatment
  1. Correction of refractive error if present.
  2. Occlusion: The child should wear his spectacles during occlusion.
  3. Pleoptics Treatment: (means full sight) It is designed to encourage foveal stimulation and to develop full vision in an amblyopic eye with eccentric fixation after occlusion of the squinting.
  4. Orthoptics Treatment: (means straight sight) It is designed to encourage the development of binocular vision by training exercises on the synoptophore (not to increase the power of the muscles).
