Uveitis · Accommodation

Range and Amplitude of Accommodation

Range of accommodation

It is the distance between the far and the near points.

Far point (Punctum Remotum)

  • Is the distant point from the eye, at which objects appear clear with accommodation completely relaxed (at infinity in the emmetropic eye).

Near point (Punctum Proximum)

  • Is the nearest point to the eye at which objects appear clear with accommodation fully exerted.

Amplitude (power) of accommodation

It is the difference between the dioptric power of the eye at rest (i.e. Focused for far point) and when accommodation is fully exerted (focused for near point).
