Syndromes of Unknown Etiology & Secondary Iridocyclitis

Syndromes of unknown etiology

  1. Bechet's syndrome: Clinically: A characteristic triad of:
    • a) Bilateral recurrent Iridocyclitis.
    • b) Recurrent Oral mucocutaneous ulcerations.
    • c) Recurrent Genital mucocutaneous ulcerations.
  2. Heterochromic iridocyclitis of Fuchs: iridocyclitis with depigmentation of the iris.
  3. Vogt-Koyanagi-Harada syndrome:
    • a) Bilateral uveitis.
    • b) Vitiligo (patchy skin depigmentation).
    • c) And poliosis (greying of eyebrows and lashes).

B. Secondary iridocyclitis

  • Keratitis (especially corneal ulcers).
  • Scleritis and episcleritis.
  • Ocular trauma.
  • Intraocular tumors.
  • Lens displacement.
  • Retinal detachment.

Secondary iridocyclitis follows inflammation, trauma, tumors or displacement of intraocular structures of the eye itself — unlike primary iridocyclitis, which is infective or non-infective (allergic / autoimmune) in origin.
