Uveitis · Iris

1) Anatomy of the Iris — Gross Anatomy

a) Site & b) Shape

  • Site: It is the anterior part of the uveal tract (it is a diaphragm dividing the space between anterior and posterior chamber).
  • It is present between the cornea and the lens.
  • Shape: It is a thin circular pigmented disc perforated at its center by the pupil.

c) Color

  • At birth: Blue color due to absence of pigment.
  • Later, it varies from grey to brown color depending on the amount of pigment in the iris stroma.

d) Surfaces

  • 1) Anterior surface: Is not a smooth surface, it has a special pattern (Iris pattern): is the appearance of fine irregularities due to the presence of:
    • The collarette: Is an irregular circular line near the papillary margin.
    • Iris crypts: Are dark depressions (between the iris root and collarette).
  • 2) Posterior surface: Is darkly pigmented.

e) Borders

  • 1) Pupillary border: Is a free border.
  • 2) Ciliary border (root of the iris): Is attached to the middle of the anterior surface of the ciliary body.
