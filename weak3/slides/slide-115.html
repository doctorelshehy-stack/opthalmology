Microscopic anatomy: the retinal layers

The retina is formed of the following layers from outside inwards (Fig.12-1):

Layer Composition
1) Retinal pigment epithelium One layer of pigmented cubical cells.
2) Photoreceptors Rods and cones. It is the light sensitive layer.
3) Outer limiting membrane It is made of outer ends of Muller's fibers.
4) Outer nuclear layer It is made of nuclei of rods and cones.
5) Outer plexiform layer Synapses between photoreceptors nuclei and bipolar cells.
6) Inner nuclear layer It is made of bipolar cells and cells of Muller’s.
7) Inner plexiform layer Synapses between bipolar and ganglion cells.
8) Ganglion cell layer
9) Nerve fiber layer It is made of axons of ganglion cells which form optic nerve.
10) Inner limiting membrane It is made of inner ends of Muller's fibers.
