Glaucoma · Primary Open Angle Glaucoma

2) Glaucomatous Cupping of the ONH

Early (suggestive) vs Late (sure) signs

ONH is examined by
  • 1) Direct ophthalmoscope (Most commonly used).
  • 2) Slit lamp biomicroscopy.
  • 3) Stereoscopic O.D. photography.
  • 4) Optical coherence tomography (OCT).
  • 5) Nerve fiber layer analyzer.
  • 6) Confocal laser ophthalmoscope (CLD).
ONH in POAG
Early (suggestive) signs Late (sure) signs
1) Larger C/D ratio more than 0.4. 1) Larger deep C/D ratio more than 0.7.
2) Vertical elongation of the cup. 2) Over hanging (undermined) edge of the cup.
3) Asymmetry of C/D between 2 eyes. 3) Interruption of the retinal vessels at the edge of the cup.
4) Splinter hemorrhage at the disc margin. 4) Nasal shift of the retinal blood vessels.
5) Increased visibility of lamina cribrosa pores. —
