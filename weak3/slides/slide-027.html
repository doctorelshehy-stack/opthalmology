Glaucoma · Treatment of POAG

3) Surgical Treatment

Indications · Types

Indications
  1. Medical or laser treatment fails to control IOP.
  2. Deterioration of V.F. inspite of controlled IOP.
  3. Poor compliance (the patient is poor or negligent).
Types
1) Trabeculectomy
  • The aim is to create a new pathway for the aqueous from the anterior chamber to the sub conjunctival space to be drained.
  • To increases the success rate, antimetabolites (5 – fluorouracil & mitomycin C) could be used.
2) Setons (Valves)
  • Used in refractory glaucoma (difficult glaucoma cases) when external scarring of the filtering bleb occurs.
  • Seton is a device used to shunt aqueous through a plastic tube, placed in the anterior chamber to a more posterior site where aqueous is drained.
3) Cyclodestructive procedures
  • Several surgical procedures that reduce aqueous secretion by destroying a part of ciliary body, these are:
  • Cyclocryotherapy (using cryo probe).
  • Laser cyclophotocoagulation.
