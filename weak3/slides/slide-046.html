Uveitis · Iris

Blood Supply & Nerve Supply of the Iris

Blood supply

  • Arteries:
    • 1) Circulus arteriosus major and the arteries forming it:
      • Two long posterior ciliary arteries.
      • Seven anterior ciliary arteries.
    • 2) Circulus arteriosus minor.
  • Veins: 4 Vortex veins.

Nerve supply

  • Motor: Short ciliary nerves which contain:
    • Parasympathetic fibers: To sphincter pupillae (via the 3rd cranial nerve (oculomotor N.)).
    • Sympathetic fibers: To dilator pupillae.
  • Sensory: 2 long ciliary nerves.
