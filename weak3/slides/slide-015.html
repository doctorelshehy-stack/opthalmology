Glaucoma · Primary Infantile Glaucoma

Differential Diagnosis

1) Large cornea
  • Megalocornea.
  • High myopia.
  • Keratoglobus: which is a bilateral condition with familial incidence later in life, rupture of descemet's membrane occur → cloudy cornea.
2) Cloudy cornea

Due to:

  • Birth trauma.
  • Metabolic disorders: mucopolysaccharidosis and mucolipoidosis.
  • Rubella syndrome.
3) 2ry infantile glaucoma

As in:

  • Trauma.
  • Tumor e.g. retinoblastoma & juvenile xanthogranuloma.
  • Persistent hyperplastic primary vitreous (PHPV).
  • Retinopathy of premature (ROP).
  • Intra ocular inflammation.
  • Ectopia lentis.
