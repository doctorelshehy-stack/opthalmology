Binocular Vision — Definition, Development & Importance

Definition

Coordinated use of both eyes to produce a single visual mental impression.

Development
  • a) At 6 months: Maintained binocular fixation (as the fovea is fully developed).
  • b) At 6 years: Binocular vision with fusion is fully developed (so training of fusion is usually not successful after 6 years of age).
Importance
  • 1) Stereopsis (depth perception).
  • 2) Biocular visual field is larger than the uniocular field alone.
  • 3) Optical defects and field defects in one field (as scotoma) are masked by the other overlapping field.
Grades: There are three grades of binocular vision — simultaneous perception, fusion and stereoscopic vision (next slide).
