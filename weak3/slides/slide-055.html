1) Iridocyclitis: Definition & Primary Etiology

Definition: It is inflammation of the iris and ciliary body.

A. Primary iridocyclitis — infective vs non-infective causes

Group Infective Non-infective (allergic or autoimmune)
1) Exogenous a) Perforated corneal ulcer or wound.
b) Post-operative infection.		 1) Bacterial allergy: To tuberculo-protein or streptococcal toxin.
2) Endogenous a) Bacterial: Pyogenic organisms. Tuberculosis and syphilis.
b) Viral: Herpes simplex.
c) Fungal: Actinomycosis.
d) Protozoal: Toxoplasmosis. 		2) Non-bacterial allergy (autoimmune uveitis):
a) To lens proteins: In iritis due to traumatic or after cataract.
b) To uveal pigment: In sympathetic ophthalmitis.
c) Constitutional: Diabetes, gout and autoimmune disease
