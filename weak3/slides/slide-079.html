Nerve Supply of the Extraocular Muscles

NerveMuscles supplied
Oculomotor (3rd) nerve Superior division: SR.
Inferior division: MR, IR, and IO.
Trochlear (4th) nerve SO.
Abducent (6th) nerve LR.
3rd nerve supplies
  • 1) EOMs: SR, MR, IR and IO (i.e. all except SO and LR).
  • 2) IOMs: Sphincter papillae and ciliary muscle (i.e. all except dilator papillae).
