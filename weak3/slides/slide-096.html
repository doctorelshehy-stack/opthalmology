4) False projection (past pointing)

Definition: False orientation of objects situated in the direction of action of the paralyzed muscle.

Principle: The brain sends more nerve impulses to the paralyzed muscle to force it to contract.

Demonstration:

  1. Close the normal eye of the patient.
  2. Tell him to point quickly an object in front of him.
  3. His finger will be directed away from the object (in the direction of action of the paralyzed muscle).
