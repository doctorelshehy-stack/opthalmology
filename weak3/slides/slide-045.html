Uveitis · Iris

1) Anatomy of the Iris — Minute Anatomy

  • A. Anterior endothelium:
    • A single layer of flat cells which is absent over the crypts.
    • Continuous with the endothelium on the back of the cornea.
  • B. Stroma:
    • Connective tissue fibers.
    • Blood vessels.
    • Pigmented cells.
    • Nerves.
  • C. Muscles:
    • Sphincter pupillae muscle: A circular band around the pupillary border.
    • Dilator pupillae muscle: Radially arranged muscle fibers extending from the outer edge of the sphincter to the root of the iris.
  • D. Pigmented epithelium:
    • Two (anterior and posterior) layers of pigmented epithelium which are continuous with those of the ciliary body.
