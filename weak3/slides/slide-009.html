Optic Nerve Head (ONH)

Retinal nerve fibers

1.2 million retinal ganglion cell axons pass across the scleral canal to the optic nerve head (optic disc).

The distribution as in (Fig. 9-4):

  • 1 . Papillomacular bundle.
  • 2-3 . Superior & inferior arcuate.
  • 4-5 . Upper and lower retina.
  • 6 . Nasal retina.
  • 7 . Within the optic nerve head.
The optic disc
  • Shape: rounded and has a physiological cup in the center with a cup/disc ratio (C/D ratio) of 0.3.
  • Size: 1.5mm in diameter.
  • Color: pale pink in color and well-defined edge.
  • Neuro-retinal rim: it is the area between the cup and outer edge of the disc. it contains the nerve fibers.
