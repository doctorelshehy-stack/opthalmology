B. Intermittent ACG (Prodromal / Subacute)

Attacks that break spontaneously

Intermittent pupillary block with acute increase of IOP that spontaneously broken, the angle is reopened and the IOP decreases.

The attack is precipitated by
  • Physical mydriasis (darkness)
  • Shallow anterior chamber.
  • Emotional stress of fear.
Symptoms
  • Transient blurring of vision and haloes (corneal edema).
  • Eye ache & frontal headache, that broken by miosis (light).
  • During the attack the eye is not congested and in between is completely normal.
Provocative test
  • Idea is to initiate pupillary dilatation either by mydriatic or dark room.
  • Rise of IOP by 8 mmHg or more after dilatation is diagnostic.
Treatment

Laser peripheral iridotomy.

Fate: Without treatment some eyes develop acute or chronic stages.
