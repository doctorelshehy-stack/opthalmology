Glaucoma: Definition & Classification

Congenital · Primary · Secondary

Definition

It is a progressive optic neuropathy with elevation of the IOP as major risk → optic nerve damage & visual field defect.

Classification of Glaucoma
  1. Congenital or developmental.
  2. Acquired glaucoma:
    • a) Primary glaucoma: with no obvious ocular disease or anomaly.
      → Open angle glaucoma (chronic simple glaucoma): Primary open angle glaucoma.
      → Closed angle glaucoma (angle closure glaucoma).
    • b) Secondary glaucoma: secondary to ocular disease.
      → It may be open angle or closed angle.
